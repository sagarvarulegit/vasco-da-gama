MASTER_STYLE_02. 50mm lens, intimate two-shot in a wooden longboat, grey humid daylight, 27 August 1498. In the captain's longboat towing astern of the Portuguese flagship SHIP_SG01 off Calicut, gaunt travel-worn DIOGO_01 with reddened eyes presses a narrow dried palm-leaf strip inscribed with dense iron-pen characters into the hands of commander VASCO_01, whose salt-cracked thumb traces the script. Around them, six freed Malabar prisoners in white cotton climb down into waiting shore boats while two sailors steady an upright carved stone padrao pillar; the smaller Bérrio SHIP_B01 rides at anchor behind on a calm grey sea. European historical oil painting, brittle palm fiber, wet gunwale wood, chafing rope, sweat on skin, no text beyond the illegible script marks, no paper scrolls, no modern boats.
