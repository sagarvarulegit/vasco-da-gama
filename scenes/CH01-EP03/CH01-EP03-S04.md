## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Departing St Helena on 16 November, the fleet reaches the Cape on 18 November but encounters fierce contrary southeasterly gales, forcing days of dangerous offshore tacking in towering seas.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Where the Atlantic meets the Indian Ocean, violent gale-force squalls and massive standing swells earned the region its original name: *Cabo das Tormentas* (Cape of Storms).

## Reconstruction

[RECONSTRUCTION] Helmsmen lashed to the whipstaff to prevent being washed overboard; green water cascading across the decks; masts groaning at their breaking point.
