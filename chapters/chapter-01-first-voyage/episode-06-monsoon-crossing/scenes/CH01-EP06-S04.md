## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Scurvy was a constant companion of the expedition across long open-sea stretches. The *Roteiro* vividly recounts crew members suffering from swollen feet and legs, and gums growing over their teeth so severely that they could not eat, requiring surgical trimming of dead tissue to survive.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] In 1498, the cause of scurvy (vitamin C deficiency) was completely unknown. European sailors attributed it to corrupt air, salted beef, or stale water. Paulo da Gama was historically renowned among the crew for his compassion, spending his personal stores and time nursing the sick.
