## Source Facts

[SOURCE] The Sheikh (local governor/sultan) of Mozambique visits the flagship in state. He is dressed in fine velvet and silk embroidered with gold thread, wearing a silk-embroidered turban and a jeweled scimitar. When Vasco presents the expedition's trade goods (red wool caps, brass bells, striped coarse cloths, and pewter basins), the Sheikh and his courtiers look on with open disdain, expecting gold, silver, and fine European luxuries. The Sheikh realizes the Portuguese are impoverished Christians, not wealthy Muslim merchants.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] King Manuel I of Portugal had equipped the armada with cheap trinkets suitable for bartering with West African coastal communities, utterly failing to anticipate that the Indian Ocean trade network was far wealthier and more sophisticated than contemporary Lisbon.
