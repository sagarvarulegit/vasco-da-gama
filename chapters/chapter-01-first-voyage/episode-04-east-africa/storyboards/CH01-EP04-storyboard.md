### Panel 1: S01 — The Silent Ordeal (Cold Open)
* **Visual:** Cramped chiaroscuro interior of *São Gabriel*’s lower hold; Vasco and Paulo tending to an exhausted mariner.
* **Focal Point:** Swinging brass grease lamp casting amber glow across curved oak ribs.
* **Color Palette:** Warm amber lantern glow, deep shadow, damp weathered oak.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 2: S02 — The Land of Good People (Rio do Cobre)
* **Visual:** Lush sub-tropical African coastline; friendly local inhabitants trading freshwater and copper rings.
* **Focal Point:** Exchange of copper rings and fresh water on the riverbank.
* **Color Palette:** Lush green mangroves, warm golden sand, sparkling azure river water.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 3: S03 — Careening at Rio dos Bons Sinais
* **Visual:** Wide shot; *São Rafael* careened on a sandbank in the Zambezi Delta with mariners scraping hulls.
* **Focal Point:** The tilted wooden hull and smoke from boiling pitch cauldrons.
* **Color Palette:** Muddy delta green, warm golden afternoon haze, dark tar smoke.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 4: S04 — The Silk Robes of Quelimane
* **Visual:** Medium two-shot; Vasco da Gama inspecting fine silk damask and turban cloth worn by East African noblemen.
* **Focal Point:** The vibrant woven silk patterned fabric held between hands.
* **Color Palette:** Royal crimson and gold silk, weathered leather doublet, tropical river background.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 5: S05 — Sighting Mozambique Island
* **Visual:** Epic widescreen establishing shot; the white coral-stone citadel of Mozambique Island surrounded by Arab dhows.
* **Focal Point:** Lateen-sailed Arab dhows gliding across the turquoise lagoon toward the Portuguese caravels.
* **Color Palette:** Dazzling coral white, deep turquoise lagoon, white triangular sails.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 6: S06 — The Fatal Audience with the Sultan
* **Visual:** Tense interior/quarterdeck shot; the Sultan of Mozambique looking in disdain at cheap red woolen caps.
* **Focal Point:** The Sultan's gold-embroidered robes vs. the cheap brass hawk-bells on the tray.
* **Color Palette:** Opulent silk gold, dull brass, dark wooden deck shadows.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 7: S07 — Ambush in the Mangrove Shallows
* **Visual:** High-drama action shot; Portuguese longboats ambushed by archers in the mangrove shallows.
* **Focal Point:** Flying arrows skipping across turquoise water as Portuguese crossbows return fire.
* **Color Palette:** Bright tropical sunlight, dark mangrove foliage, splashing white water.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 8: S08 — Thunder in the Lagoon (Departure & Cliffhanger)
* **Visual:** Master dramatic shot; *São Gabriel* surging north through billowing white smoke in the lagoon under golden light.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco on the quarterdeck pointing northward as sails catch the monsoon breeze.
* **Color Palette:** Billowing white-gray smoke, glowing sunset gold, deep turquoise ocean.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.
