## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 2 March 1498 the fleet arrives at Mozambique Island. The journal records a flourishing island town with houses of stone and mortar, fine mosques, and crowded roadsteads filled with Arab and Swahili lateen-rigged dhows carrying gold, silver, cloves, ginger, pearls, and rubies.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Mozambique Island was a key southern emporium of the Swahili Sultanate of Kilwa and Indian Ocean trade. The Portuguese encountered a sophisticated Islamic maritime civilization that had traded with India, Arabia, and Persia for centuries.
