---

## 2. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **VOICEOVER PROFILE:**  
  Somber, poignant, emotionally gripping narration capturing the catastrophic cost of European oceanic expansion.
* **LAYERED AMBIENCE TRACKS:**  
  1. Dead calm silence with subtle buzzing heat and sluggish water lap.
  2. Dark wooden hold acoustics with labored breathing and creaking timber.
  3. Roaring ocean headwinds and crashing sea spray.
  4. Roaring bonfire flames with crackling oak and hissing ocean water.
* **FOLEY FX:**  
  Canvas shroud sliding into water with a heavy splash, tearing juicy orange peels, stone chisel taps on limestone, collapsing burning mast crashing into waves.
* **MUSICAL SCORE:**  
  Mournful solo cello, Gregorian funeral chants, tragic minor choral themes, transitioning into uplifting acoustic warm flutes at Malindi, before ending on a heartbreaking tragic crescendo.

## 3. Continuity Requirements & Verification Checklist

* **CREW DEBILITATION:**  
  Crew appearance reflects catastrophic physical collapse: emaciated faces, hollow eyes, bruised arms, ragged clothing.
* **FLEET STATUS:**  
  *São Rafael* (`SHIP_SR01`) is officially burned and permanently removed from the sailing fleet at Mtangata; remaining crew consolidated into *São Gabriel* (`SHIP_SG01`) and *Bérrio* (`SHIP_B01`).
