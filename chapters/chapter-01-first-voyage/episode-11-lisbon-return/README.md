## 1. Scope & Historical Narrative

Episode 11 concludes the epic 26-month journey of the 1497–1499 Portuguese expedition to India:

1. **Arrival of the Bérrio (10 July 1499):** Nicolau Coelho sails into the Tagus River, bringing the electrifying news that India has been found and spices secured.
2. **The Flagship Comes Home (Late August 1499):** *São Gabriel* enters Belém with its precious cargo of pepper, cinnamon, and cloves.
3. **The Mourning Commander (September 1499):** Vasco da Gama steps ashore at Restelo alone, dressed in black mourning robes, visiting the Hermitage of Belém where he prayed before departure.
4. **The Triumphal Procession (18 September 1499):** Lisbon erupts in magnificent pageantry; crowds cheer as exotic Indian spices, silk banners, and Malabar hostages are paraded through the city.
5. **Audience with King Manuel I:** In the Palace of Alcáçova, Vasco kneels before the King, presenting the Zamorin's palm-leaf letter, Indian gemstones, and introducing his advisor Gaspar da Gama.
6. **The 54 Survivors:** The roll call of the expedition: only 54 of the 170 original crew members returned alive, having completed a journey of over 24,000 nautical miles.
7. **The Shockwave in Europe & Venice:** News of the direct maritime route sends shockwaves across the continent, shattering the centuries-old Venetian-Mamluk monopoly on the global spice trade.
8. **The Epilogue: Stone and Legacy:** The founding of the monumental Jerónimos Monastery at Belém, financed by the Indian spice tax, marking the birth of the first truly global maritime empire.
