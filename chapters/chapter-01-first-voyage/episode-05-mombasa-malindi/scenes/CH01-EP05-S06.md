## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 15 April 1498 (Easter Sunday): The fleet arrives before Malindi, a city seated on a plain close to the shore, with stone and mortar houses and extensive palm groves. Malindi is the bitter political and commercial rival of Mombasa. The King of Malindi sends a knight with gifts of large fat sheep, cloves, ginger, nutmeg, and pepper, offering complete friendship and promising to furnish a reliable pilot to India.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] The Swahili coast was deeply fragmented into competing city-states. Malindi seized upon the arrival of the formidable Portuguese fleet as a powerful military and commercial counterweight against its aggressive southern neighbor, Mombasa.
