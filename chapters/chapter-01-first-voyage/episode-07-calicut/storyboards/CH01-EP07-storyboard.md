### Panel 1: S01 — The First Words in India
* **Visual:** Medium two-shot inside the beach warehouse; João Nunes meeting the Tunisian Moor Monçaide.
* **Focal Point:** Stark contrast between the convict's rough clothes and the Moor's silk robes.
* **Color Palette:** Warm tropical sunlight, golden jute spice sacks, dusty market rays.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 2: S02 — The Tears of Discovery
* **Visual:** Emotional wide shot on *São Gabriel*; sailors weeping as Monçaide proclaims the wealth of Calicut.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco da Gama's face of overwhelmed relief; hands clasped in prayer.
* **Color Palette:** Morning azure sea, golden ship deck, emerald Malabar coastline.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 3: S03 — The Royal Procession through Calicut
* **Visual:** Vibrant tracking shot of Vasco in a teak palanquin escorted by Nair warriors and blaring horns.
* **Focal Point:** Athletic Nair swordsmen marching amidst thousands of cheering Indian citizens.
* **Color Palette:** Saffron, crimson silk curtains, lush green palms, terracotta roof tiles.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 4: S04 — The Mistaken Altar of Mary
* **Visual:** Atmospheric chiaroscuro interior; Portuguese officers praying before a Hindu temple altar.
* **Focal Point:** Golden oil lamps reflecting on stone carvings of deities; priests applying holy ash.
* **Color Palette:** Deep granite shadow, warm amber lamp flames, blue incense smoke.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 5: S05 — The Splendor of the Zamorin
* **Visual:** Master wide shot of the throne room; the Zamorin reclining on green velvet draped in emeralds.
* **Focal Point:** Solid gold spittoon, shimmering diamonds, Vasco bowing in steel armor.
* **Color Palette:** Imperial emerald green, brilliant gold, pure white muslin.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 6: S06 — The Disaster of the Cheap Gifts
* **Visual:** Humiliating medium shot; Arab merchants and royal factors laughing at brass basins and wool caps.
* **Focal Point:** Da Gama's clenched fists and burning gaze in the background.
* **Color Palette:** Dreary grey morning light, dull brass reflections, dark stone warehouse.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 7: S07 — The Interrogation
* **Visual:** Tight psychological confrontation; the Zamorin questioning Vasco as storm lightning strikes outside.
* **Focal Point:** Cold gaze of the King vs. intense defiance of Vasco da Gama.
* **Color Palette:** Dark shadows, cold electric blue lightning, flickering torch orange.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 8: S08 — Hostages at Pandarani
* **Visual:** Master cliffhanger shot; iron gates locking the Portuguese in as storm rains pour down.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco trapped behind iron bars; distant caravels rolling in offshore storm waves.
* **Color Palette:** Slate gray monsoon rain, sizzling yellow torchlight, dark wet stone.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.
