* **Characters:** `COELHO_01`, Lisbon dockworkers, port officials, cheering citizens.
* **Ships:** `SHIP_B01` (Bérrio) dropping anchor on the calm Tagus waters.
* **Environment:** Bright summer morning sun, golden reflections on the Tagus River, cheering crowds on the sandy shore of Restelo.
