## 1. Scope & Historical Narrative

Episode 5 depicts the fleet's dramatic transition from the hostile Swahili barrier of Mombasa to the pivotal diplomatic alliance at Malindi, concluding with securing the master navigator who will guide them across the Indian Ocean to India:

1. **The Grounding on the Shoals of São Rafael (5 April 1498):** Escaping Mozambique, *São Rafael* runs aground at low tide on uncharted coral shoals off Mtangata; the crew labors in tropical heat to refloat the hull at high tide.
2. **Arrival at Mombasa (7 April 1498, Palm Sunday):** The armada arrives before the imposing white coral-stone citadel of Mombasa. The Sultan offers gifts of citrus and sheep, attempting to lure the ships inside the harbor.
3. **The Convicts' Reconnaissance & Cultural Collision (8 April 1498):** Two Portuguese convicts (*degredados*) tour Mombasa and visit Gujarati merchants (*Banians*), mistaking Hindu/Jain religious shrines and icons for Eastern Christian worship.
4. **Panic in the Harbor Entrance (10 April 1498):** As *São Gabriel* attempts to enter, she misses the channel near the reef and drops anchor with a thunderous roar. Believing their ambush is exposed, the Swahili pilots and envoys jump overboard into the sea.
5. **Midnight Sabotage & Interrogation (11–12 April 1498):** Night swimmers attempt to sever the ships' anchor cables. Interrogating captured Moors with boiling oil drops reveals the Sultan's plot to slaughter the fleet.
6. **Arrival at Malindi (15 April 1498, Easter Sunday):** The fleet sails north to Malindi, a rival port to Mombasa. The Sultan welcomes the Portuguese warmly, offering fresh provisions, spices, and mutual respect.
7. **The Floating Royal Summit (18 April 1498):** Vasco da Gama and the Sultan of Malindi meet in royal rowboats on the water with trumpets, cannon salutes, and exchange of gifts.
8. **The Master Navigator & The Open Sea (22–24 April 1498):** The Sultan provides an expert Gujarati pilot (`PILOT_01`, *Malemo Canaqua*) skilled in celestial navigation (*kamal*). Vasco erects the *Padrão de São Rafael*, and the armada launches into the monsoon sprint across the Arabian Sea.
