### SCENE 8: CH01-EP08-S08 — The Mysterious Spy: Gaspar da Gama (24 September 1498)
* **Location:** Quarterdeck of `SHIP_SG01` anchored off Anjediva Island.
* **Source Basis:** *Roteiro* provides detailed account of the European-dressed merchant arriving, speaking Venetian, and being tortured and captured as a spy.
* **Historical Confidence:** HIGH.
* **Characters:** `GASPAR_01` (Gaspar da Gama, middle-aged Polish Jew in fine linen), `VASCO_01`, Portuguese guards.
* **Ships:** `SHIP_SG01`.
* **Environment:** Sunset over the sheltered lagoon of Anjediva; long shadows across the wooden deck.
* **Classification:** [SOURCE] Eyewitness account of the spy's arrival and interrogation; [HISTORICAL] Jewish merchant networks across Venice and the Indian Ocean; [RECONSTRUCTION] Initial conversation; [CINEMATIC] Intriguing psychological encounter setting up his baptism.
* **Narration & Voiceover:**
  > *"Then, out of the tropical mist, a small rowboat approached. A tall, elegant man stepped aboard, greeting the Portuguese in fluent Venetian. He claimed to be a Christian merchant serving the Sultan of Bijapur. But Da Gama smelled a spy. Subjected to fierce interrogation, the man confessed: he was a Polish Jew from Poznań who had crossed Egypt to India. Captivated by his encyclopedic knowledge of Asian trade, Vasco spared his life and baptized him as 'Gaspar da Gama'—a man who would become the chief advisor to the Portuguese crown."*
