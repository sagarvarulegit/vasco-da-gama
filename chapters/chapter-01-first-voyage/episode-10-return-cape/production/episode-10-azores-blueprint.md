# Episode Blueprint: Chapter 1 Episode 10
## Rounding the Cape & The Death of Paulo da Gama (1499)

**Episode ID:** `CH01-EP10`  
**Working Title:** *The Price of Glory: The Death of Paulo da Gama & The Lonely Conqueror*  
**Historical Period:** February – August 1499  
**Primary Source:** *A Journal of the First Voyage of Vasco da Gama, 1497–1499* (ed. E. G. Ravenstein, Hakluyt Society, 1898, pp. 95–105)  
**Target Duration:** ~8:00 to 9:00 min  
**Style Identifier:** `MASTER_STYLE_01`  

---

## 1. Episode Narrative Architecture & Retention Beats

```
  0:00 - 0:20 ──► [THE HOOK] Two surviving ships; triumph at Good Hope; the tragic death of Paulo in the Azores.
  0:20 - 0:45 ──► [MAP TRANSITION] Portolan route doubling the Cape and racing north across Atlantic.
  0:45 - 2:00 ──► [ACT I: COASTING SOUTH] Two lone ships fighting headcurrents along southern Africa (S01).
  2:00 - 3:15 ──► [ACT II: THE CAPE CONQUERED] Doubling the Cape of Good Hope on 20 March (S02).
  3:15 - 4:15 ──► [ACT III: THE 27-DAY SPRINT] Flying north through trade winds under full sail (S03).
  4:15 - 5:15 ──► [ACT IV: SPLIT IN THE MIST] Bérrio speeds ahead into the fog; São Gabriel alone (S04).
  5:15 - 6:15 ──► [ACT V: ILLNESS AT SANTIAGO] Paulo's lungs collapse; chartering the caravel (S05).
  6:15 - 7:15 ──► [ACT VI: THE RACE FOR AZORES] Racing north across the chilly North Atlantic (S06).
  7:15 - 8:45 ──► [ACT VII: TRAGEDY & FUNERAL] The death of Paulo (S07); solitary mourning in the crypt (S08).
```

---

## 2. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **VOICEOVER PROFILE:**  
  Deeply poignant, tragic, respectful historical narration highlighting the personal human sacrifice behind imperial conquest.
* **LAYERED AMBIENCE TRACKS:**  
  1. Roaring South Atlantic winds and breaking coastal surf at the Cape.
  2. Steady trade wind hiss and humming rigging.
  3. Muffled oceanic sea fog with dripping moisture.
  4. Echoing Romanesque stone monastery crypt with rain on roof tiles.
* **FOLEY FX:**  
  Sails snapping full of wind, shallow gasping breaths, heavy stone tomb slab sliding and booming shut, echoing footsteps in monastery.
* **MUSICAL SCORE:**  
  Triumphant maritime brass fanfares for Good Hope, transitioning into sorrowful Portuguese fado-inspired guitar, tragic solo cello, and an emotional choral Requiem.

---

## 3. Continuity Requirements & Verification Checklist

* **VASCO_01 Continuity:**  
  Transitions from hardened oceanic captain into a grief-stricken brother in black mourning cowl; face worn by 2 years of unimaginable ordeal.
* **PAULO_01 Resolution:**  
  Solemn death and burial at the monastery of São Francisco in Angra do Heroísmo, Terceira Island (Azores).
