## Source Facts

[SOURCE] The storeship (SHIP_ST01) is stripped of all provisions, sails, anchors, cordage, and timber. Her stores are distributed among São Gabriel, São Rafael, and Berrio, and her hull is burned on the beach.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Scurvy and casualties had thinned the crews. Burning the supply ship was a calculated, irreversible commitment—burning their bridges to ensure the survival of the remaining three fighting ships.

## Reconstruction

[RECONSTRUCTION] The roaring inferno lighting up the night, silhouette of the ship's charred ribs like a dying sea monster, sailors watching in somber reverence.
