## Source Facts

[SOURCE] The *Roteiro* notes that the fleet experienced strong following winds that propelled them across the ocean in only 23 days—an exceptionally fast crossing for the era, but one demanding constant vigilance in turbulent seas.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] In May, the Southwest Monsoon produces sustained Force 7 to 8 gales in the central Arabian Sea. Ships could cover up to 150 nautical miles per day running before the wind, but the extreme rolling placed immense strain on oak hulls and standing rigging.
