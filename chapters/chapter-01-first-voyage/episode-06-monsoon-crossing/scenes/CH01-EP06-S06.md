## Source Facts

[SOURCE] On Friday, 18 May 1498, after 23 days without seeing land, the mariners sighted floating reeds, green river weeds, and numerous sea snakes in the water. The pilot declared that they were approaching land.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] The monsoon rivers of Kerala and the Western Ghats wash enormous amounts of river grass, reeds, and organic debris miles out to sea into the Arabian Sea. Pelagic sea snakes (*Hydrophiinae*) proliferate in coastal Malabar waters, serving as an unmistakable navigational sign for Indian Ocean sailors.
