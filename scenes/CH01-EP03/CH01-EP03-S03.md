## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Cultural misunderstanding turns to violence; dozens of armed warriors appear on the ridge; stones and horn-tipped spears rain down; Vasco da Gama is struck and wounded in the leg as the longboats fight to escape.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Fear and misinterpretation of gestures triggered an ambush. Crossbowmen in the boats fired into the melee to prevent the entire landing party from being overrun.

## Reconstruction

[RECONSTRUCTION] Red blood staining the sea-foam, Vasco roaring through agony while holding the gunwale, the whistle of deadly wooden assegais slicing through spray.
