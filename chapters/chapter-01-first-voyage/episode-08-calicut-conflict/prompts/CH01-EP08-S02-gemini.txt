MASTER_STYLE_02. 35mm lens, interior daylight medium shot, warm slatted sunlight, July 1498. Inside the Portuguese merchandise house at Calicut, Malabar Coast, India. Portuguese factor DIOGO_01 (plain worn linen shirt and wool hose, ink-stained fingers) leans over a brass balancing scale beside a Malayali spice merchant in a simple white cotton dhoti with sandalwood forehead markings, tipping whole black peppercorns from a coarse woven scoop onto the brass pan, his pouring wrist steady. Dias squints against the glare, one hand raised to shield his eyes. Hot still air thick with pepper dust. Background of stacked coarse sacks, folded striped cloth and copper vessels. European historical oil painting, polished brass, woven fiber, matte peppercorns, no text, no modern objects.
