# CH01-EP03 — Cape of Good Hope

**Status:** In Production — Scene Drafting & Prompt Pack Creation

**Series structure:** Chapter 1 — First Voyage; Episode 3 — Cape of Good Hope

## Scope

The expedition’s arrival on the southern African coast, rounding of the Cape of Good Hope, and entry into the Indian Ocean (November–December 1497):
- Landfall and refitting at St Helena Bay (*Angra de Santa Helena*).
- First encounters with local Khoikhoi inhabitants and the skirmish on the strand.
- Battling severe contrary gales and rounding the Cape of Good Hope on 22 November 1497.
- Pastoralist trade and flute music at Mossel Bay (*Angra de São Brás*).
- Planned dismantling and burning of the supply ship (`SHIP_ST01`).
- Departure of the three surviving ships into the Indian Ocean.

## Historical guardrails

- Grounded directly in the surviving *Roteiro* (Ravenstein 1898 edition).
- Accurately depict the Khoikhoi inhabitants without European colonial stereotypes: pastoralists with fat-tailed sheep/cattle, skin cloaks (*karosses*), shell ornaments, and reed flutes (*pastorais*).
- Depict the weather accurately: violent squalls, heavy contrary gales, and high swell around the Cape turning point.
- Show progressive weathering on ships (`SHIP_SG01`, `SHIP_SR01`, `SHIP_B01`, `SHIP_ST01`) and crew (sun-darkened skin, sea-salt stained clothing, unkempt beards).
- Clearly classify source facts, historical context, reconstructions, and cinematic framing.
