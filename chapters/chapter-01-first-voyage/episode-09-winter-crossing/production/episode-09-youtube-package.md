October 1498. Having secured precious spices and escaped Calicut, Vasco da Gama’s three surviving wooden ships set sail for Portugal. But without an experienced Indian Ocean pilot, the return voyage across the Arabian Sea turns into the deadliest catastrophe of the expedition.
