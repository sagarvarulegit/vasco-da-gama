## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Ilha do Sal is sighted on 22 July; three vessels are found; the fleet reunites with the captain-major on 26 July after a period of calm.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Rejoining a separated fleet was a navigational and emotional event, not a scripted triumph.
