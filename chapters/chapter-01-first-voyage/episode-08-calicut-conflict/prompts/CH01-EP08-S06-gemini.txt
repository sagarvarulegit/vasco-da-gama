MASTER_STYLE_02. 24mm lens, wide environmental naval shot, glassy noon calm under a towering black squall wall, 30 August 1498. Three weathered Portuguese ocean-going ships, flagship SHIP_SG01, SHIP_SR01 and the smaller Bérrio SHIP_B01, each with deep wooden hull, high sterncastle, square-rigged masts and lateen mizzen, becalmed with limp sails on glassy grey water a league off Calicut. A wide arc of some seventy low wooden shore boats rows toward them, crews in red folded-quilted cotton cuirasses bending over long oars. One bronze muzzle-loader on the flagship's rail exhales a ribbon of white-black powder smoke. A gunner with cloth-wrapped ears flinches; distant oarsmen glance up at the boiling cloud. European historical oil painting, glassy swell, scorched bronze, wet sailcloth, no gunports, no explosions, no modern ships.
