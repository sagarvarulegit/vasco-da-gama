MASTER_STYLE_02. 35mm lens, medium-wide interior night shot, warm oil-lamp light against humid darkness, late May 1498. Inside a rough rest-house on the road from Calicut to the Pandarani anchorage, Malabar Coast, India. In the foreground, Portuguese commander VASCO_01 (late thirties, dark hair, short beard, worn wool doublet and linen shirt, travel-stained cloak, salt-cracked knuckles) bends over a rough wooden table writing a letter by a clay oil lamp, jaw locked rigid. Around him stand impassive Calicut officials with bare torsos, fine cotton waistcloths and gold earrings, one tapping a finger on the table; two exhausted Portuguese soldiers wait by the door. Through the open doorway, moonlit masts of the flagship SHIP_SG01 ride at anchor off the dark coast. European historical oil painting, thick humid air, lamp smoke, rough palm-fiber matting, weathered teak, no text, no plate armor, no modern objects.
