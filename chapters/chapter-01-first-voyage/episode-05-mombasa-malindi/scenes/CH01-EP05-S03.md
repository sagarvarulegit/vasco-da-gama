## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 8 April 1498: Vasco da Gama sends two convicts (*degredados*) ashore to inspect Mombasa. They are escorted through the city and brought to the house of Indian Christian merchants (actually Gujarati/Banian traders), who show them an image/icon they venerate with incense. The convicts return to the ships reporting that the city is rich, well-built, and contains devout Christians.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] The Portuguese were preconditioned by the medieval myth of Prester John to find Christian communities everywhere in the Indian Ocean. In Mombasa and later in Calicut, they repeatedly mistook Hindu and Jain devotional practices and temple iconography for Eastern Christian rites.
