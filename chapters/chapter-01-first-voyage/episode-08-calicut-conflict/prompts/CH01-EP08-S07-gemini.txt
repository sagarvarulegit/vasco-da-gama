MASTER_STYLE_02. 28mm lens, wide working-beach morning shot, fierce clear sunlight, late September 1498. On a populated island beach at Anjediva, the Portuguese flagship SHIP_SG01 and the smaller Bérrio SHIP_B01, deep-hulled ocean carracks with high sterns, lie heeled on their sides with weed-furred hulls exposed, shores braced against the keels; SHIP_SR01 rides afloat beyond the reef. Shirtless sun-darkened crews scrape weed and barnacles with iron tools while water-bearers queue from a hewn-stone tank beside a ruined stone church and a small straw-roofed chapel with three dark devotion stones. Mainland sellers unload pumpkins and fish from a beached canoe; children watch from the chapel steps. European historical oil painting, weed-matted oak, rasping iron, sun glare on shallows, a worked inhabited shore, no modern objects.
