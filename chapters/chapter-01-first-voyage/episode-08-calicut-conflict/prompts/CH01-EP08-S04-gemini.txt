MASTER_STYLE_02. 35mm lens, high-angle medium-wide deck shot, hard midday sun, 19 August 1498. On the main deck of the weathered Portuguese flagship SHIP_SG01, a large ocean-going carrack with deep wooden hull, high sterncastle, two square-rigged masts and a lateen mizzen, riding at anchor off Calicut. Eighteen Malabar visitors, six men of quality in white and gold-bordered cotton and the rest merchants and boatmen, stand shocked amid coiled rigging as Portuguese sailors in plain wool and linen quietly block hatchways and rails with pikes and spanned crossbows of plain steel and wood. On the quarterdeck, commander VASCO_01 (late thirties, short beard, worn sea-stained doublet) holds one arm lowered in a flat stopping gesture, eyes narrowed against the glare. Blinding noon sun on tarred deck and calm sea. European historical oil painting, weathered oak, rusted bolts, hemp rigging, no text, no plate armor, no modern objects.
