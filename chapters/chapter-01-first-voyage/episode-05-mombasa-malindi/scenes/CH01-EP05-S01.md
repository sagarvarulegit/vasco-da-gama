## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 5 April 1498: While coasting northward at night, the *São Rafael* runs aground at low water upon a extensive coral shoal two leagues from the mainland. At daybreak, the crew inspects the reef, unloads ballast to lighten her, and at high tide successfully refloats the ship without fatal damage. The shoals are named *Baixos de São Rafael*.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Navigation along the Swahili coast was notoriously treacherous for deep-draft European naus due to extensive barrier reefs, powerful equatorial currents, and fluctuating tidal ranges up to four meters.
