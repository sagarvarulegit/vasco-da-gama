## Source Facts

[SOURCE] On Friday, 18 May 1498, the lookouts sighted high land. The pilot recognized a prominent mountain (termed *Monte Formoso* in Portuguese accounts, identified with Mount Eli near Cannanore/Kannur) and confirmed it was the coast of India. The crews celebrated with prayers and Thanksgiving.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] The Western Ghats mountain range rises precipitously over 2,000 meters above the Malabar coastal plain, serving as an unmistakable maritime beacon visible over 40 miles out to sea on clear post-monsoon afternoons.
