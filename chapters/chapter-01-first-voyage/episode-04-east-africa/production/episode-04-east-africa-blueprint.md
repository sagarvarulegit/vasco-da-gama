# CH01-EP04 — East Africa & Mozambique: The Edge of the Islamic World (Video Blueprint)

**Format:** Immersive European historical-painting docudrama video  
**Pacing Style:** Prestige OTT / Streaming Docudrama (*Shōgun*, *Black Sails*, *Master and Commander*)  
**Target Duration:** ~80 seconds (Condensed Master Cut) / 8–10 minute Extended Cut  
**Active Visual Style:** `MASTER_STYLE_02`  
**Fleet State:** 3 surviving ships (`SHIP_SG01`, `SHIP_SR01`, `SHIP_B01`) — weather-worn, fouled hulls.  
**Historical Period:** December 1497 – March 1498  
**Primary Source:** *A Journal of the First Voyage of Vasco da Gama, 1497–1499* (ed. E. G. Ravenstein, pp. 15–35)

---

## Narrative Architecture

The episode transforms the viewer from a passive observer into an active crew member aboard the expedition. It follows an escalating emotional arc:
* **Act I: The Visceral Struggle (S01–S03)** — Scurvy, decaying bodies, brutal physical labor careening ships on tropical sandbars.
* **Act II: The Golden Threshold (S04–S05)** — Transition from the wild unknown to the opulent, white-sailed Islamic trading civilization of the Swahili Coast.
* **Act III: The Fatal Climax (S06–S08)** — Cultural misunderstanding, the insult of cheap European trinkets, ambush in the mangroves, and an explosive naval artillery duel before fleeing north into the unknown.
