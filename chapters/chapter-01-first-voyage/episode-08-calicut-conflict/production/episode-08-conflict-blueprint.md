## 2. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **VOICEOVER PROFILE:**  
  High-tension, gritty documentary delivery capturing the rapid escalation from commercial diplomacy to open naval warfare.
* **LAYERED AMBIENCE TRACKS:**  
  1. Choppy Malabar coastal roadstead surf with wind in shrouds.
  2. Aromatic spice warehouse acoustics with merchant murmurs and scale clinks.
  3. Restrained bombard exchange under dead calm, then gale and rain — no triumphal destruction staging.
  4. Populated Anjediva ambience (chapel, tank crews, sellers' canoes, lagoon birds) — no paradise-emptiness, no waterfalls.
* **FOLEY FX:**
  Quill and barred doors, hatch battens, pike butts on oak, single bombard detonation rolling over water, iron scrapers on weed-furred hulls, palm fiber handled.
* **MUSICAL SCORE:**
  Rabeca and frame drum under tension scenes; bansuri with mridangam ashore; chenda with shawm at the pursuit; wooden flute with lute at Anjediva; solo lute dissolving into drone for the stranger. No sitar, taiko, orchestral battle tropes, or fretted-guitar textures.

## 3. Continuity Requirements & Verification Checklist

* **VASCO_01 Continuity:**
  Worn practical maritime clothing throughout, sun-darkened and salt-stained; no plate armor (unmarked in the account); hard watchful demeanor.
* **GASPAR_01 Character Design:**
  About forty per the account; plain dusty linen, cloth touca, belted sword; origins disputed (LOW) — no kaftan, no admiral's uniform, no baptism staging at Anjediva.
* **SHIP CONTINUITY:**
  All 3 surviving ships (`SHIP_SG01`, `SHIP_SR01`, `SHIP_B01`); weed-fouled hulls accumulating since the Atlantic; only `SHIP_SG01` and `SHIP_B01` hove down at Anjediva, `SHIP_SR01` afloat.
