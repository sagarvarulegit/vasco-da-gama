## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Two local coastal headmen/merchants visit the ships in dugouts; one wears a headdress with silk-embroidered fringe, and the other wears a cap of green satin. Through signs, they indicate that large vessels from distant lands often visit this coast carrying precious goods. Vasco erects the stone pillar (*Padrão de São Rafael*) on a low sandspit before departing and names the river *Rio dos Bons Sinais* (River of Good Omens).

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] This was the psychological turning point of the voyage: the first tangible evidence that they had reached the outer trading fringe of the Indian Ocean and the Swahili-Arab trade network connecting Africa to Arabia and India.
