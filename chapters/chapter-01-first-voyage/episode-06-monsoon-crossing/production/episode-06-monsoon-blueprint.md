## 2. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **VOICEOVER PROFILE:**  
  Authoritative, immersive cinematic documentary narration. Deep baritone, measured cadence, strict <= 10.0 seconds per scene clip (18–22 words).
* **LAYERED AMBIENCE TRACKS:**  
  1. Open Arabian Sea winds, shifting from moderate breeze to violent Force 8 howling squalls.
  2. Rushing ocean hull friction, frothing bow wave wakes, and bioluminescent nocturnal water hissing.
  3. Tropical rain pelting heavy linen canvas and oak decks.
  4. Calm Malabar coastal surf, rhythmic dipping of Indian wooden oars, and shore cicadas.
* **FOLEY FX:**  
  Creaking wooden knees and masts under torque, taut knotted kamal cord clicking, rattling heavy iron anchor chains, splashing rainwater in casks, sliding brass spyglass.
* **MUSICAL SCORE:**  
  Hybrid medieval Portuguese modality blended with authentic Indian Ocean and Malabar instruments (santur, bansuri flute, framed frame drums, mridangam, low cello drones, and soaring victory choir).

## 4. Continuity Requirements & Verification Checklist

* **VASCO_01 Continuity:**  
  Appearance shows severe wear: deeply sunburnt, salt-weathered skin, dark beard slightly longer, worn dark woolen and leather garments with salt water stains. Wears polished steel breastplate in final scene (S08).
* **PILOT_01 Continuity:**  
  Authentic Gujarati/Indian Ocean attire: simple clean cream cotton tunic, draped white turban, barefoot on deck, holding wooden *kamal* instrument.
* **SHIP CONTINUITY:**  
  - `SHIP_SG01` (São Gabriel): Main flagship, square-rigged, weathered oak planking, visible sea patina.
  - `SHIP_SR01` (São Rafael): Companion square-rigged nau, showing patched sails and heavy sea grime.
  - `SHIP_B01` (Bérrio): Sleek, swift lateen-rigged caravel, leading the fleet through heavy swells.
* **GEOGRAPHIC CONTINUITY:**  
  Transition from arid Kenyan coastline across deep Arabian Sea to emerald tropical Western Ghats and coconut palm beaches of Malabar (Kappad / Calicut).
