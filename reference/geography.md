# Geographic Continuity

## Route spine

Lisbon/Belém → Cape Verde region → South Atlantic → Bay of St Helena → Cape of Good Hope → Natal and southeast African coast → Rio dos Bons Signaes/Quelimane area → Mozambique Island → Mombasa → Malindi → Arabian Sea → Calicut/Kozhikode and Pandarani → return across the Arabian Sea → East African coast → Cape/Atlantic → Portugal.

## Place rules

- **Lisbon/Belém:** Tagus estuary, royal/port infrastructure, Atlantic Iberian climate.
- **Cape of Good Hope:** exposed southern African coast, powerful swell, wind and cold variability.
- **Mozambique/Mombasa/Malindi:** Swahili coast with established Muslim trading cities and Indian Ocean exchange; do not collapse them into one generic “African” setting.
- **Calicut/Kozhikode:** major Malabar trading port in a dense political and commercial network; humid monsoon environment, local institutions, merchants, and religious sites.

All scene locations must be checked against the master timeline. A visual transition cannot imply impossible travel timing or geography.
