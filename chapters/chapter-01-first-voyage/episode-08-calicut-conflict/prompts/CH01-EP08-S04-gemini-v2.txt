MASTER_STYLE_02. 35mm lens, high-angle medium-wide deck shot, hard midday sun, 19 August 1498. On the main deck of the weathered Portuguese flagship SHIP_SG01, a large ocean-going carrack with deep wooden hull and high sterncastle, riding at anchor off Calicut. Eighteen Malabar visitors from the Kerala coast, six men of quality and the rest merchants and boatmen, ALL with bare torsos, white cotton waistcloths, gold earrings and oiled topknot hair, absolutely no turbans and no long robes, stand shocked amid coiled rigging as Portuguese sailors in dark wool doublets, linen shirts and round wool caps, none wearing turbans, quietly block hatchways and rails with plain wooden pikes and a few spanned crossbows. On the quarterdeck, commander VASCO_01, late thirties with short dark beard in a worn sea-stained doublet, holds one arm lowered flat, eyes narrowed against the glare.
