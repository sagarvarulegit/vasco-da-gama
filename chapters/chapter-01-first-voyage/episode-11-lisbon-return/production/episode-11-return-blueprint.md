# Episode Blueprint: Chapter 1 Episode 11 (Series Grand Finale)
## The Triumphal Return to Lisbon & The Birth of an Empire (1499)

**Episode ID:** `CH01-EP11` (Series Grand Finale)  
**Working Title:** *The Voyage That Changed the World: Return to Lisbon & The Birth of an Empire (1499)*  
**Historical Period:** July – September 1499 & Epilogue  
**Primary Source:** *A Journal of the First Voyage of Vasco da Gama, 1497–1499* (ed. E. G. Ravenstein, Hakluyt Society, 1898, pp. 105–140)  
**Target Duration:** ~9:30 to 10:30 min  
**Style Identifier:** `MASTER_STYLE_01`

```
  0:00 - 0:25 ──► [THE GRAND FINALE HOOK] 26 months; 24,000 miles; 54 survivors; the world transformed forever.

  0:50 - 2:00 ──► [ACT I: THE FIRST SHIP HOME] Bérrio drops anchor in the Tagus; crowds roar (S01).
```

## 2. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **VOICEOVER PROFILE:**  
  Grand, transcendent, majestic historical narrative climax with deep philosophical depth.
* **LAYERED AMBIENCE TRACKS:**  
  1. Tagus River waters with cheering crowds and roaring church bells across Lisbon.
  2. Reverberant Gothic royal throne room with hushed courtier murmurs.
  3. Bustling Venetian Grand Canal with echoing murmurs and St. Mark's bells.
  4. Majestic ocean breeze on the Belém cliffs with crashing Atlantic waves.
* **FOLEY FX:**  
  Caravel anchor splashing, heavy spice sacks thudding on stone, horse hooves on cobblestones, heavy gold collar clinking, rustling palm-leaf letters.
* **MUSICAL SCORE:**  
  Triumphant Portuguese royal fanfares, Renaissance court strings, soaring cathedral choir, moving solo trumpet tribute, and a colossal cinematic full orchestral finale fading into the timeless sound of the sea.

## 3. Continuity Requirements & Verification Checklist

* **FULL-CIRCLE NARRATIVE CONTINUITY:**  
  Scene S03 directly echoes Episode 1 Scene S04 (the beach of Restelo and the Hermitage of Belém), bringing emotional closure to the 2-year expedition.
* **CASUALTY & CREW ACCURACY:**  
  Preserves the exact primary source numbers: 170 departed, 54 returned, 116 dead, 2 ships surviving.
