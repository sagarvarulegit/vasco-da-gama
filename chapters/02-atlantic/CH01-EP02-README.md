## Journal relationship

The surviving first-voyage journal/account directly records the post-departure route: sighting the Canaries on 15 July, separation in fog, sighting Sal in the Cape Verde region on 22 July, reunion and becalming, arrival at São Thiago on 27 July for meat, water, wood, and yard repairs, departure on 3 August, the captain-major’s broken main yard on 18 August, birds and a whale on 22 August, further whales/seals on 27 October, signs of land on 1 November, and landfall on 4 November. The account is not Vasco’s personal diary; it is the surviving expedition journal/account.
