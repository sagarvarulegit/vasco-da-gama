## Source Facts

[SOURCE] At the river mouth, many men fell gravely ill from prolonged ocean exposure and lack of fresh food. The captain-major and his brother tended personally to the crew.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Having spent seven continuous months without fresh land produce, the crew suffered from severe physical exhaustion and scurvy. The personal dedication of Vasco and Paulo da Gama to nursing the crew was crucial in maintaining discipline and morale.
