### Panel 1: S01 — The Glass Ocean
* **Visual:** Wide establishing shot; caravels sitting dead in the water under a blazing sun; heat shimmer on the horizon.
* **Focal Point:** Limp white sails hanging from yardarms over glassy sea.
* **Color Palette:** Blinding white-yellow sun, pale bleached blue sea, heat haze.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 2: S02 — The Horror of the Black Sickness
* **Visual:** Cramped chiaroscuro interior of *São Gabriel*'s hold; Vasco and Paulo tending to dying sailors.
* **Focal Point:** Swaying lantern illuminating swollen gums, bruised limbs, and pale faces.
* **Color Palette:** Warm amber lantern glow, deep shadow, damp weathered oak.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 3: S03 — The Blood-Red Sunset Burial
* **Visual:** Tragic medium shot at sunset; canvas shroud sliding into the purple sea as Paulo recites prayers.
* **Focal Point:** The weighted shroud splashing into the water against glowing clouds.
* **Color Palette:** Blood-red and purple sunset, dark ocean swell, black silhouettes.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 4: S04 — The Crucifix in the Storm
* **Visual:** High-drama shot on the quarterdeck; Vasco raising a crucifix as weeping crew beg to turn back.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco's iron gaze and the crucifix catching blue lightning.
* **Color Palette:** Indigo storm clouds, electric blue lightning, white wave spray.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 5: S05 — The Citadel of Mogadishu
* **Visual:** Wide shot of the high Somali cliffs with white stone minarets; Portuguese bombards firing broadsides.
* **Focal Point:** Billowing cannon smoke plumes and bright orange muzzle flashes.
* **Color Palette:** Dazzling coral white, desert sand ochre, deep cobalt sea.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 6: S06 — The Citrus Miracle of Malindi
* **Visual:** Heartwarming medium shot in Malindi lagoon; sailors eating fresh oranges with tears of joy.
* **Focal Point:** Bright orange citrus fruit and weeping mariners breaking into smiles.
* **Color Palette:** Vibrant orange, lush tropical green palms, sparkling turquoise water.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 7: S07 — The White Pillar of Malindi
* **Visual:** Heroic low-angle shot; the limestone Padrão standing proudly against the sky on the headland.
* **Focal Point:** The carved stone cross and royal arms overlooking the ocean.
* **Color Palette:** Crisp white limestone, bright azure sky, deep blue ocean.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 8: S08 — The Midnight Pyre of São Rafael
* **Visual:** Master tragic wide shot; *São Rafael* ablaze in the night sea; Paulo da Gama weeping on a boat.
* **Focal Point:** Colossal orange flames consuming the ship and reflection on black water.
* **Color Palette:** Brilliant fiery orange and red, deep midnight black, starry sky.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.
