## Source Facts

[SOURCE] The *Roteiro* records that the fleet navigated under the direct guidance of the pilot engaged at Malindi, who directed the ships across the open northern ocean without sighting land for over three weeks.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Arab, Persian, and Indian navigators had perfected the *kamal*—a simple yet sophisticated rectangular wooden card with a knotted cord held between the teeth. By aligning the lower edge with the horizon and the upper edge with Polaris, each knot marked the exact latitude of known ports (such as Calicut), unaffected by the ship's roll.
