# SHIP_ST01 — Supply ship

**Reference status:** APPROVED / CANONICAL BASELINE for Chapter 1; disputed later name intentionally unresolved (24 August 2026)

- **Type:** stores/transport vessel used for provisions and equipment on the outward leg.
- **Identity uncertainty:** often assigned a name in later summaries (including São Miguel), but the name and exact fate require source-level confirmation; use SHIP_ST01 until locked.
- **Continuity silhouette:** utilitarian, heavily laden, less prestigious than the principal ships.
- **Condition arc:** Provisioned outward leg; fully unloaded, broken up, and burned at São Brás (Mossel Bay, South Africa) on 7–8 December 1497 as recorded in the *Roteiro* (Ravenstein p. 11), with stores and crew distributed among São Gabriel, São Rafael, and Bérrio.
