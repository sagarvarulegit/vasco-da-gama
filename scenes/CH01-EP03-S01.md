## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 4 Nov land sighted; 7 Nov fleet anchors in St Helena Bay. Crew beaches and careens ships, scrapes hulls, mends sails, and fetches wood and water.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] 120 days at sea had infested hulls with shipworm and thick barnacle crusts. If not careened and pitch-sealed here, the imminent Cape gales would splinter the hulls.
