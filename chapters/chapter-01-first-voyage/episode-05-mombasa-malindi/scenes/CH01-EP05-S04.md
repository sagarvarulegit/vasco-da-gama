## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 10 April 1498: Vasco da Gama attempts to sail his ships into the harbor of Mombasa. As the flagship *São Gabriel* enters the channel, she refuses to answer the helm and drifts dangerously close to a shoal. Vasco orders the sails struck and the anchor let go. Hearing the clamor and the anchor drop, the Swahili pilots aboard and two visiting Moors take fright, believing their plot has been uncovered, and leap into the sea, scrambling into waiting native boats.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Harbor entrances on coral-fringed islands were classic ambush zones; if a European vessel grounded at the mouth, she was helpless to boarders and could be plundered with impunity.
