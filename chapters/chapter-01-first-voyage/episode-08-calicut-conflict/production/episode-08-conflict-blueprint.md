## 2. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **VOICEOVER PROFILE:**  
  High-tension, gritty documentary delivery capturing the rapid escalation from commercial diplomacy to open naval warfare.
* **LAYERED AMBIENCE TRACKS:**  
  1. Choppy Malabar coastal roadstead surf with wind in shrouds.
  2. Aromatic spice warehouse acoustics with merchant murmurs and scale clinks.
  3. Deafening naval battle cannonade with screaming squalls and splintering wood.
  4. Peaceful tropical paradise ambience at Anjediva (gentle waves, jungle birds, mountain waterfalls).
* **FOLEY FX:**  
  Clinking iron chains, crossbow triggers clicking into place, deafening cannon blasts with heavy sub-bass impact, rasping iron hull scrapers on oak wood.
* **MUSICAL SCORE:**  
  Thunderous battle percussion (chenda, taiko, timpani) paired with European war horns, transitioning into an enigmatic Venetian lute melody and suspenseful minor drone for Gaspar da Gama.

## 3. Continuity Requirements & Verification Checklist

* **VASCO_01 Continuity:**  
  Wears full steel plate armor and leather jerkin during the hostage crisis and naval battle; tactical, unyielding demeanor.
* **GASPAR_01 Character Design:**  
  Distinguished 50-year-old Polish Jewish merchant-admiral; silver-streaked dark beard; sharp observant eyes; fine European-cut linen kaftan; high-class bearing.
* **SHIP CONTINUITY:**  
  All 3 surviving caravels (`SHIP_SG01`, `SHIP_SR01`, `SHIP_B01`) depicted with barnacle-encrusted hulls before careening at Anjediva.
