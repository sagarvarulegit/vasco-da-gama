## 2. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **VOICEOVER PROFILE:**  
  Grand documentary narrator with subtle dramatic tension. Emphasizes the deep cultural collisions and diplomatic misunderstandings of the late 15th-century Indian Ocean world.
* **LAYERED AMBIENCE TRACKS:**  
  1. Bustling Malabar marketplace with authentic Malayalam vocalizations and port activities.
  2. Reverberant stone temple acoustics with rhythmic Vedic chants, gongs, and oil lamp crackles.
  3. Opulent palace interior with courtyard fountains and royal attendant movements.
  4. Torrential monsoon downpour and howling coastal gales at Pandarani.
* **FOLEY FX:**  
  Clinking steel armor plates, creaking bamboo palanquins, clattering cheap brass basins on stone floor, rustling gold-threaded muslin, heavy iron gate slamming shut with echo.
* **MUSICAL SCORE:**  
  Fusion of authentic Malabar temple percussion (chenda, ilathalam), classical Indian veena/sitar melodies, and dark European orchestral brass creating palpable suspense.

## 3. Continuity Requirements & Verification Checklist

* **VASCO_01 Continuity:**  
  10 months at sea: deeply tanned, weathered face, short dark beard. Wears crimson velvet doublet and polished steel armor for ceremonial diplomacy in India.
* **ZAMORIN_01 Continuity:**  
  Historically grounded depiction of the Samutiri Raja: fine white gold-bordered Malabar muslin (*kasavu*), large emerald ear pendants, ruby and diamond rings, regal composure.
* **HISTORICAL CULTURAL ACCURACY:**  
  - No generic "Arabian" or "Mughal" architecture.
  - Authentic Kerala architecture: sloping clay-tiled roofs, carved wooden pillars, granite temple mandapas, and coconut palm landscapes.
  - Nairs depicted authentically: athletic bare-chested warriors with topknots, white dhotis, steel broadswords, and round brass shields.
