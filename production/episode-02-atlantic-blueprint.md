## Episode Objective

Bring the audience aboard the four vessels during the longest continuous open-ocean navigation undertaken in European history up to that time (over three months out of sight of land). The episode visualizes the technical realities of Atlantic navigation: the *volta do mar* (the vast westward arc to catch southern westerlies), the danger of fog separation, constant physical maintenance, broken spars, ocean wildlife encounters, and the quiet tension of searching for signs of the African coast before reaching St Helena Bay.

---
