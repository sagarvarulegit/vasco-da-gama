# Production Roadmap

1. Lock source bibliography, chronology notes, and uncertainty register.
2. Lock character references (VASCO_01 first; then named officers, pilot, and local figures).
3. Lock ship silhouettes and condition sheets.
4. Build Chapter 1 storyboard (8–15 source-supported scenes).
5. Generate one controlled image at a time; review historical grounding and continuity.
6. Approve and lock Chapter 1 before proceeding to Chapter 2.

No final artwork is canonical until it passes both historical review and continuity review.
