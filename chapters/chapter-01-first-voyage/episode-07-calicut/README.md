## 1. Scope & Historical Narrative

Episode 7 chronicles the monumental first contact between Western Europe and the Malabar Coast of India at Calicut (Kozhikode):

1. **The First Contact Ashore (21 May 1498):** The convict *degredado* João Nunes lands at Calicut and meets Tunisian Moors speaking Castilian. Their opening exchange enters world history: *"May the Devil take thee! What brought you hither?"* — *"We come in search of Christians and spices."*
2. **Monçaide Boards the Flagship (22 May 1498):** The Moor Monçaide returns to *São Gabriel*, proclaiming the vast riches of Calicut to weeping, overjoyed Portuguese sailors.
3. **The Palanquin Procession (28 May 1498):** Vasco da Gama and 13 chosen officers land in ceremonial finery, carried through packed streets in covered palanquins escorted by hundreds of armed Nair warriors and blaring bronze horns.
4. **The "Virgin Mary" Temple Collision (28 May 1498):** The Portuguese enter a major Hindu temple (*pagoda*), mistaking Hindu deities for Eastern Christian saints and praying before Brahmin priests.
5. **Audience with the Zamorin (28 May 1498):** Vasco meets the Samutiri Raja of Kozhikode reclining on green velvet cushions, draped in pearls and emeralds, chewing betel nut (*paan*) from a golden spittoon.
6. **The Fatal Trade Gift Disaster (29 May 1498):** The royal treasurer and Arab merchants inspect Vasco’s intended gifts (striped cloth, brass basins, coral beads, butter) and laugh in ridicule, refusing to present such cheap trinkets to the King.
7. **The Cold Second Audience (30 May 1498):** The Zamorin interrogates Da Gama: *"If you come from a rich king, why did you bring no gold or silver?"*
8. **Detention at Pandarani (31 May – 2 June 1498):** Vasco da Gama is detained at Pandarani under armed guard amidst monsoon rains, while the fleet offshore prepares its cannons for war.

## 2. Fleet & Character Continuity

* **Armada (3 Vessels):**
  * `SHIP_SG01` (*São Gabriel* — Flagship, anchored in the roadstead of Pandarani / Calicut)
  * `SHIP_SR01` (*São Rafael* — Paulo da Gama in command offshore)
  * `SHIP_B01` (*Bérrio* — Nicolau Coelho in command of armed ship's boats)
* **Character Continuity:**
  * `VASCO_01` (Vasco da Gama): Dressed in rich crimson velvet doublet and polished steel breastplate for royal diplomacy, contrasting with his weathered, sun-darkened face.
  * `ZAMORIN_01` (Samutiri Raja of Kozhikode): Powerful Hindu ruler of Calicut, clad in fine white gold-threaded Malabar cotton (*kasavu*), adorned with massive emerald earrings, diamond rings, and ruby necklaces.
  * `CATUAL_01` (Chief Judicial Officer / Minister of the Palace): Authoritative Malabar noble escorting the Portuguese party.
  * `MONCAIDE_01` (Tunisian Moor merchant): Fluent in Castilian, acting as initial broker and informant.
  * `NUNES_01` (João Nunes): Portuguese *degredado* (convict scout) sent ashore first.
