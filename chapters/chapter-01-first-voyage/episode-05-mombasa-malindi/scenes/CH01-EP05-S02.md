## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 7 April 1498 (Eve of Palm Sunday): The armada arrives before Mombasa, a large city seated on an island, with stone and mortar houses and a fortified harbor. The Sultan of Mombasa sends a boat laden with large sweet oranges, lemons, sugar cane, and a sheep, sending a ring as a pledge of friendship and inviting the Portuguese to bring their vessels into the safe inner harbor.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Mombasa was one of the wealthiest Swahili sultanates on the Zanj coast, controlling extensive trade in ivory, gold, and timber. It possessed strong maritime defenses and sophisticated coral-rag stone architecture.
