## 1. Scope & Historical Narrative

Episode 10 depicts the two remaining vessels navigating the South African coastline, rounding the Cape of Good Hope, the separation of the fleet, and the tragic death of Paulo da Gama in the Azores:

1. **Southward along East Africa (Feb 1499):** *São Gabriel* and *Bérrio* make swift progress south, passing Mozambique and Mossel Bay.
2. **Rounding the Cape of Good Hope (20 March 1499):** The two ships double the Cape of Good Hope with favorable southern winds, re-entering the Atlantic Ocean.
3. **The 27-Day Atlantic Run (April 1499):** Carried by southeastern trade winds, the vessels fly northward for 27 straight days without shortening sail.
4. **The Separation of the Fleet (25 April 1499):** Off the shoals of Rio Grande / Cape Verde, *Bérrio* under Nicolau Coelho separates from the flagship and races ahead to Lisbon.
5. **Crisis at Cape Verde (May–June 1499):** Anchored at Santiago, Paulo da Gama's health collapses from pulmonary illness and severe physical exhaustion.
6. **The Race for the Azores (July 1499):** Vasco charters a fast caravel, leaving *São Gabriel* behind under João de Sá, rushing his dying brother toward the fresh climate of the Azores.
7. **The Death of Paulo da Gama (August 1499):** Landing at Angra do Heroísmo on Terceira Island; despite emergency medical care, Paulo da Gama dies in Vasco's arms.
8. **The Funeral at São Francisco (August 1499):** Vasco buries his brother in the monastery of São Francisco in Angra; overcome with grief, Vasco remains in solitary mourning while Portugal celebrates the greatest triumph in its history.
