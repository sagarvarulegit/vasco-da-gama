## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Dense fog causes Paulo da Gama to lose sight of the captain-major and the other vessels; the ships make for Cape Verde as instructed if separated.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Fleet cohesion depended on visibility, signals, and prearranged contingencies.

## Reconstruction

[RECONSTRUCTION] Exact fog density, signal sequence, and deck reactions.

## Cinematic Interpretation

[CINEMATIC] Visibility collapses until the fleet becomes sound and memory.
