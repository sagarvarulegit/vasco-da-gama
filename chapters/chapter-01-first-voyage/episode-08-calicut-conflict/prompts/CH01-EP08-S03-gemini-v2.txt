MASTER_STYLE_02. 35mm lens, medium-wide interior shot, tense thin-bladed afternoon light, 13 August 1498. Inside the shuttered Portuguese merchandise house at Calicut, India. Factor DIOGO_01, a lean young Portuguese clerk with dark hair and light beard, wearing clearly European dress, a plain undyed linen shirt with sleeves, dark wool hose and a simple short doublet, stands among stacked trade goods and barred wooden shutters, one hand frozen over an unsent paper letter. The Zamorin's Nair watchmen with plain straight swords and round hide shields, bare torsos, white cotton waistcloths and topknot hair, take post at the doors. Through the doorway, dark-robed Mecca merchants pass without turning. Stifling heat, dust in light blades. European historical oil painting, European Taylor-cut clothing sharply distinct from local wrap garments, no local dress on the Portuguese factor, no chains, no text, no modern objects.
