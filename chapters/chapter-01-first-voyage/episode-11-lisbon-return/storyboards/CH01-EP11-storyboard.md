### Panel 1: S01 — The First Ship Home
* **Visual:** Wide shot; *Bérrio* dropping anchor in the sparkling Tagus River as cheering crowds rush the Restelo beach.
* **Focal Point:** Nicolau Coelho waving from the quarterdeck as anchor splashes.
* **Color Palette:** Summer gold sunlight, bright blue Tagus water, Lisbon's green hills.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 2: S02 — Sacks of Gold: The Spices of India
* **Visual:** Medium-wide shot; sacks of black pepper and cinnamon unloaded from *São Gabriel* onto stone quays.
* **Focal Point:** Royal scribes and customs clerks inspecting exotic spices in awe.
* **Color Palette:** Golden afternoon light, deep brown burlap, black pepper reflections.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 3: S03 — The Solitary Walk at Restelo
* **Visual:** Emotional wide shot; Vasco da Gama in dark black mourning robes walking alone across the empty wet sand toward the Hermitage.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco's solitary figure and long shadow on the beach where thousands wept in Episode 1.
* **Color Palette:** Melancholic golden-orange sunset, deep black robe, wet sand reflections.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 4: S04 — The Triumphal Procession
* **Visual:** Monumental street shot; Vasco riding a dark warhorse through tapestry-draped Lisbon streets showered in flower petals.
* **Focal Point:** Cheering crowds, blaring trumpets, chests of spices, and Malabar envoys.
* **Color Palette:** Crimson tapestries, royal gold, white rose petals, bright sun.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 5: S05 — The Crown of Discovery
* **Visual:** Opulent throne room shot; Vasco kneeling before King Manuel I, presenting the Zamorin's palm-leaf letter.
* **Focal Point:** King Manuel placing the heavy gold chain over Vasco's neck.
* **Color Palette:** Cathedral light through stained glass, royal ermine and crimson, gold.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 6: S06 — The Roll Call of the 54
* **Visual:** Somber medium shot; the 54 scarred, surviving mariners standing in formation receiving royal honors.
* **Focal Point:** Weathered, tear-streaked veteran faces remembering their 116 dead comrades.
* **Color Palette:** Golden hour sun, weathered wool tunics, bronze medals.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 7: S07 — The Panic of the Doge: Venice
* **Visual:** High-drama shot on the Venetian Rialto; patricians dropping diplomatic letters in shock over the Grand Canal.
* **Focal Point:** Panicked Venetian merchants realizing their spice monopoly is destroyed.
* **Color Palette:** Overcast grey canal, dark damask velvet, cold marble arches.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 8: S08 — The Stone Monument of Belém (Series Climax)
* **Visual:** Timeless epic finale wide shot; Vasco on the Belém cliffs watching new armadas sail into the sunset beneath the grand Manueline arches of the Jerónimos Monastery.
* **Focal Point:** The soaring stone monument, the setting sun over the Atlantic, and the dawn of global history.
* **Color Palette:** Radiant purple-and-gold sunset sky, white Manueline limestone, deep azure ocean.
* **Duration:** 25–30 sec.
