# Vasco da Gama — First Voyage

Historical comic / animated visual storytelling project focused on the 1497–1499 expedition.

Project instructions and continuity rules: [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)

AI-agent continuation workflow: [AGENT_WORKFLOW.md](AGENT_WORKFLOW.md)

Current stage: Chapter 1 Episode 1 & Episode 2 assembled videos and thumbnails available for review.

## Project map

- `reference/` — historical sources, timeline, geography, visual styles, characters, ships, uncertainties
- `chapters/` — chapter-level scope and status
- `scenes/` — source-grounded scene records and image links
- `images/` — locked stills and approved thumbnail artwork
- `storyboards/` — storyboard assembly and continuity sheets
- `prompts/` — image-to-video prompts and generation instructions
- `production/` — episode blueprints, reviews, approvals, locks, and export notes
- `videos/` — assembled episode video files, organized by episode
- `research/` — additional research notes and source extracts
