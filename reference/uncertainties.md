# Uncertainty Register

- **Journal authorship:** anonymous surviving manuscript; Álvaro Velho is traditional attribution, not settled fact. João de Sá and other possibilities appear in scholarship/editorial history.
- **Manuscript date:** surviving copy is generally dated to the first half of the sixteenth century; it is not the lost original shipboard document.
- **Vasco’s appearance:** no secure contemporary portrait for the 1497–1499 voyage; all facial design is reconstruction.
- **Crew totals and deaths:** numbers vary across accounts and editorial calculations; do not hard-code a total until the relevant source passage is checked.
- **Supply ship identity/fate:** later summaries may name it São Miguel, but the project retains SHIP_ST01 until primary-source verification.
- **Ship design:** tonnage, rigging, stern forms, and paint details are incompletely documented; references use cautious type-level reconstruction.
- **Named officers’ scene presence:** Paulo da Gama, Nicolau Coelho, and Bartolomeu Dias have documented associations, but exact day-by-day presence and dialogue must be sourced per scene.
- **Malindi pilot:** role is recorded; personal identity, origin, language, and appearance are uncertain.
- **Local architecture and dress:** scene-specific research is required for Mozambique, Mombasa, Malindi, and Calicut; never use generic “African,” “Arabian,” or “Indian” visual shorthand.
- **Return chronology:** individual ships returned at different times and in different condition; the 1499 endpoint should remain ship-specific.
