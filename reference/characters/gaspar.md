# GASPAR_01 — Gaspar da Gama (The Merchant-Admiral of Anjediva)

**Reference status:** APPROVED / CANONICAL BASELINE (1498–1499)

- **Role:** Polish Jewish merchant, former admiral/advisor to the Sultan of Bijapur; captured at Anjediva, baptized as Gaspar da Gama, and became chief strategic advisor to the Portuguese crown.
- **Age:** Approximately late 40s to early 50s during first encounter (1498).
- **Face/build:** Tall, dignified, European-Mediterranean facial structure, silver-streaked dark beard, sharp, observant, highly intelligent eyes.
- **Skin/hair:** Weathered Mediterranean skin tone; silvering dark hair and trimmed beard.
- **Clothing:** Refined late-15th-century Levantine/Venetian style linen kaftan, fine wool tunic, leather riding boots; shifts to Portuguese courtly attire upon return to Lisbon in 1499.
- **Languages/Tools:** Fluent in Venetian Italian, Spanish, Arabic, Persian, and Hebrew; possesses encyclopedic knowledge of Indian Ocean maritime trade routes and coastal politics.
- **Personality cues:** Cultured, polyglot, strategic diplomat, calm under pressure, deeply knowledgeable.
- **Known facts:** Eyewitness capture recorded in the *Roteiro*; subsequently sailed with Pedro Álvares Cabral (1500) and Vasco da Gama's second voyage (1502).
- **Uncertain/speculative:** Exact early biographical trajectory prior to reaching the Red Sea and India.
