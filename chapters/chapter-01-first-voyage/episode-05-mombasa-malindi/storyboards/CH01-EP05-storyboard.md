## Scene-by-Scene Storyboard Panel Specifications

### Panel 1: `CH01-EP05-S01` — The Reef of São Rafael (Cold Open)
* **Visual Framing:** 24mm wide angle, low water-level composition.
* **Palette:** Blinding equatorial noon, crystalline turquoise water, exposed brown coral shelves, dark listing wooden hull.
* **Focal Point:** Crew waist-deep in shallows hauling kedge anchor; Paulo da Gama shouting from the listing rail.
* **Audio Transition:** Breathless, tense silence broken by splash of wading feet, strained hemp rope, and jarring wooden groan against coral.

### Panel 2: `CH01-EP05-S02` — The White Citadel of Mombasa
* **Visual Framing:** 35mm wide shot, three-quarter deck view.
* **Palette:** Luminous morning sun, deep emerald sea, brilliant whitewashed coral limestone walls, lush green palms.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco da Gama and Nicolau Coelho looking cautiously down at Swahili envoys presenting baskets of sweet oranges.
* **Audio Transition:** Arabic oud and delicate woodwinds underlaid by an ominous cello drone.

### Panel 3: `CH01-EP05-S03` — The Convicts' Shore Reconnaissance
* **Visual Framing:** 50mm eye-level interior, chiaroscuro lighting.
* **Palette:** Cool stone shadows, warm shafts of sunlight, blue sandalwood incense smoke, cream and gold cotton garments.
* **Focal Point:** Two rough Portuguese convicts crossing themselves before a sacred Indian icon in a merchant's courtyard.
* **Audio Transition:** Meditative tanpura and flute harmonies layered with faint Latin choral chants.

### Panel 4: `CH01-EP05-S04` — Panic in the Harbor Channel
* **Visual Framing:** 28mm wide high-kinetic action angle.
* **Palette:** Overcast tropical sea, white reef breakers, dark timber deck, flying sea spray.
* **Focal Point:** Massive iron anchor crashing into the water; two Swahili pilots in mid-air leaping over the wooden rail.
* **Audio Transition:** Sudden explosive burst of taiko war drums and screeching brass dissonance.

### Panel 5: `CH01-EP05-S05` — Midnight Sabotage & The Drop Torture
* **Visual Framing:** 35mm nocturnal chiaroscuro composition.
* **Palette:** Pitch-black darkness, glowing orange brazier coals, smoky yellow torchlight, gleaming damp wood.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco da Gama standing over the iron brazier; watchmen with lit matchlocks aiming into the dark sea.
* **Audio Transition:** Deep brooding bass drone, crackle of torches, and cold metallic iron strikes.

### Panel 6: `CH01-EP05-S06` — Arrival at the Palm-Fringed Haven of Malindi
* **Visual Framing:** 24mm wide panoramic aerial/water-level sweep.
* **Palette:** Radiant Easter morning, vibrant turquoise lagoon, golden beaches, emerald palm groves, white stone city.
* **Focal Point:** *São Gabriel* and *São Rafael* surrounded by welcoming Swahili outriggers offering spices.
* **Audio Transition:** Majestic uplifting orchestral theme with African kora, flutes, and soaring strings.

### Panel 7: `CH01-EP05-S07` — The Floating Royal Summit
* **Visual Framing:** 35mm eye-level two-boat water tableau.
* **Palette:** Sparkling midday lagoon, crimson damask parasol, gold and green silk robes, drifting white cannon smoke.
* **Focal Point:** The Sultan of Malindi on his cushioned throne greeting Vasco da Gama standing in crimson velvet.
* **Audio Transition:** Regal fanfare blending European silver trumpets with resonant carved ivory *siwa* horn calls.

### Panel 8: `CH01-EP05-S08` — The Master Navigator & The Open Sea (Climax)
* **Visual Framing:** 35mm dynamic three-quarter quarterdeck view looking east.
* **Palette:** Sapphire-blue deep ocean, foaming whitecaps, billowing square sails with red crosses, green headland with white stone pillar.
* **Focal Point:** Gujarati pilot `PILOT_01` measuring the stars with the wooden *kamal* alongside Vasco da Gama.
* **Audio Transition:** Grand symphonic crescendo with roaring monsoon winds and thundering timpani.
