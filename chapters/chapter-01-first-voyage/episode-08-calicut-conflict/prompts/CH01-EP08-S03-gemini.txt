MASTER_STYLE_02. 35mm lens, medium-wide interior shot, tense thin-bladed afternoon light, 13 August 1498. Inside the shuttered Portuguese merchandise house at Calicut, India. DIOGO_01 (gaunt factor in worn linen, hand frozen mid-gesture over an unsent letter) stands among stacked trade goods and barred wooden shutters while the Zamorin's Nair watchmen with plain straight swords and round hide shields, bare torsos and white cotton waistcloths, take post at the doors, thumbs hooked in waistbands. Through the doorway, dark-robed Mecca merchants pass without turning. Stifling heat, dust hanging in light blades. European historical oil painting, sun-baked laterite, iron rust, rough cotton, no text, no chains, no modern objects.
