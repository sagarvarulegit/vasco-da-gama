## Source Facts

[SOURCE] On Tuesday, 24 April 1498, the Portuguese armada weighed anchor and departed Malindi for India. The King of Malindi had provided an experienced pilot (traditionally recorded as a Gujarati mariner) to guide the fleet across the Arabian Sea directly to Calicut.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] The Indian Ocean monsoon system governs all long-distance trade between Africa, Arabia, and India. The summer Southwest Monsoon (May to September) blows steadily northeastward, enabling a rapid deep-sea passage in under a month.
