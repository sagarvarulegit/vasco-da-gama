## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Fleet reaches the Cape on 18 Nov but encounters fierce contrary southeasterly gales, forcing days of dangerous offshore tacking in towering seas.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Where the Atlantic meets the Indian Ocean, violent gale-force squalls and massive standing swells earned the region its original name: Cabo das Tormentas (Cape of Storms).
