## 2. YouTube Video Description

```text
April 1498. After escaping the hostile harbor of Mozambique, Vasco da Gama’s battered three-ship armada arrives before the imposing white coral citadel of Mombasa. Lured by gifts of citrus and promises of safe anchorage, the Portuguese edge toward a lethal harbor trap—only for a midnight sabotage and a dramatic interrogation to uncover the conspiracy.

Fleeing northward, the fleet reaches Malindi, Mombasa's bitter rival. On the neutral water of the turquoise lagoon, Gama and the Sultan of Malindi forge a historic royal alliance. In return, the Sultan delivers the decisive key to the expedition: an elite Gujarati navigator wielding the kamal celestial instrument, ready to guide the armada across 23 days of open monsoon seas to Calicut.

Based strictly on the primary eyewitness journal (Roteiro da primeira viagem de Vasco da Gama, ed. E.G. Ravenstein, Hakluyt Society, 1898).
```
