### SCENE 1: CH01-EP07-S01 — First Contact: "Christians and Spices" (21 May 1498)
* **Location:** Calicut beachfront and the house of Tunisian Moors.
* **Source Basis:** *Roteiro* records sending the convict João Nunes ashore; he is brought to two Tunisian Moors who greet him in Castilian.
* **Historical Confidence:** HIGH.
* **Characters:** `NUNES_01` (Portuguese degredado), `MONCAIDE_01` (Tunisian Moor merchant in fine robes).
* **Environment:** Humid tropical midday, bustling Malabar coastal market filled with spice sacks, coconut timber warehouses, curious crowds.
* **Classification:** [SOURCE] The famous spoken exchange recorded in *Roteiro*; [HISTORICAL] Castilian/Genoese spoken in Indian Ocean trading ports; [RECONSTRUCTION] Interior of merchant's dwelling; [CINEMATIC] Close-up shock on both faces.
* **Narration & Voiceover:**
  > *"21 May 1498. Vasco da Gama sends a condemned convict ashore to test the waters of Calicut. The moment he steps onto the sand, two North African merchants look at him in utter disbelief, greeting him in fluent Castilian: 'May the Devil take thee! What brought you hither?' The convict’s answer would define an empire: 'We come in search of Christians and spices.'"*

### SCENE 3: CH01-EP07-S03 — The Procession of the Nairs (28 May 1498)
* **Location:** Streets of Calicut (from the beach to the city center).
* **Source Basis:** *Roteiro* describes Vasco da Gama and 13 companions carried in palanquins escorted by the Catual and hundreds of armed Nairs with swords and trumpets.
* **Historical Confidence:** HIGH.
* **Characters:** `VASCO_01` (in covered palanquin with crimson velvet drapery), `CATUAL_01` (holding a silver cane of office), armed Nair warriors.
* **Environment:** Dense tropical town, thousands of Malabar citizens lining wooden balconies and thatched roofs, loud cacophony of bronze horn blasts and drums.
* **Classification:** [SOURCE] Palanquin procession and Nair escort; [HISTORICAL] Calicut urban layout and military retinue; [RECONSTRUCTION] Density of crowd; [CINEMATIC] Slow tracking shot through a sea of spectators.
* **Narration & Voiceover:**
  > *"28 May 1498. Vasco da Gama lands with thirteen chosen officers in their finest silks and armor. Lifted into covered palanquins, they are escorted through packed streets by hundreds of Nair warriors with polished steel swords and brass bucklers. Thousands of spectators climbed onto rooftops just to catch a glimpse of the pale foreigners from the sea."*
* **Audio Direction:**

### SCENE 4: CH01-EP07-S04 — The Temple of the "White Saints" (28 May 1498)
* **Location:** Large Hindu Temple (*pagoda*) near the palace of Calicut.
* **Source Basis:** *Roteiro* explicitly describes the Portuguese entering a temple, believing it to be a Christian church, mistaking the deity for the Virgin Mary ("Mariam").
* **Historical Confidence:** HIGH.
* **Characters:** `VASCO_01`, Portuguese officers, Brahmin priests with white threads across chests applying sacred ash.
* **Ships:** None.
* **Environment:** Towering carved stone temple pillars, thousands of flickering oil lamps, swirling incense smoke, dark inner sanctum (*garbhagriha*).
* **Classification:** [SOURCE] Portuguese praying in Hindu temple believing it to be Christian; [HISTORICAL] Traditional Kerala temple architecture and Brahmin rituals; [RECONSTRUCTION] Specific prayer posture; [CINEMATIC] Chiaroscuro oil-lamp shadows playing across stone sculptures of multi-armed deities.
* **Narration & Voiceover:**
  > *"Convinced that India was ruled by the legendary Eastern Christian Emperor 'Prester John', the Portuguese were escorted into a towering stone temple. Seeing priests applying holy ash before sculpted figures, Vasco da Gama knelt before the altar, believing he was praying to the Virgin Mary. One of his sailors whispered nervously: 'If these be devils, I worship the true God.'"*
* **Audio Direction:**
  > `[VOICEOVER: ironic, intriguing, solemn whisper]`  
  > `[AMBIENCE: resonant temple bell ringing, rhythmic Vedic chanting in stone hall, crackling oil lamps]`

### SCENE 5: CH01-EP07-S05 — In the Presence of the Zamorin (28 May 1498)
* **Location:** Royal Audience Hall, Stone Palace of the Zamorin, Calicut.

* **Historical Confidence:** HIGH.
* **Characters:** `ZAMORIN_01` (Samutiri Raja), `VASCO_01`, royal pages holding gold spittoon and betel leaves (*paan*).
* **Environment:** Vast stone hall with polished plaster walls, heavy bronze chandeliers, gold basins, fragrant sandalwood and rosewater scent.
* **Classification:** [SOURCE] Eyewitness details of the Zamorin's appearance and court ritual; [HISTORICAL] Samutiri royal protocol; [RECONSTRUCTION] Exact dialogue staging; [CINEMATIC] High-contrast wide shot showcasing the staggering wealth of India.

His fingers gleamed with rubies and diamonds. As attendants offered betel nut in solid gold basins, Vasco da Gama stepped forward to represent the King of Portugal."*

### SCENE 6: CH01-EP07-S06 — The Humiliation of the Gifts (29 May 1498)
* **Location:** Palace warehouse / lodging of the Portuguese envoy.
* **Source Basis:** *Roteiro* explicitly lists the cheap gifts Vasco brought and the derisive laughter of the royal officers and Arab merchants.
* **Historical Confidence:** HIGH.
* **Characters:** `VASCO_01`, `CATUAL_01`, wealthy Arab merchant factors, Portuguese sailors.
* **Environment:** Dimly lit stone warehouse; wooden crates unpacked on the floor.
* **Classification:** [SOURCE] Exact inventory of gifts and the inspectors' contemptuous reaction; [HISTORICAL] Sophistication of Indian Ocean luxury trade; [RECONSTRUCTION] Staging of argument; [CINEMATIC] Close-up of Da Gama's burning humiliation and rage.
* **Narration & Voiceover:**
  > *"The next morning came absolute disaster. When the royal officials and Arab merchants arrived to inspect the King of Portugal's gifts, they found: twelve pieces of striped cloth, four red woolen hoods, six brass basins, and casks of sugar and butter. The inspectors burst into mocking laughter. 'The poorest merchant from Mecca offers more than this,' they sneered. 'Your King must have no gold at all.'"*
* **Audio Direction:**

### SCENE 7: CH01-EP07-S07 — The Cold Interrogation (30 May 1498)
* **Location:** Inner council room of the Palace of Calicut.
* **Source Basis:** *Roteiro* records the Zamorin questioning Vasco about his king's wealth and why he brought no gold.
* **Historical Confidence:** HIGH.
* **Characters:** `ZAMORIN_01`, `VASCO_01`, Arab merchant advisors whispering to the King.
* **Environment:** Shadowy palace room illuminated by bronze torchieres; monsoon storm lightning flashing through stone lattice windows.
* **Classification:** [SOURCE] The King's sharp questions and Vasco's defensive explanation; [HISTORICAL] Growing influence of Arab merchant factions; [RECONSTRUCTION] Body language of ministers; [CINEMATIC] Intense psychological confrontation.
* **Narration & Voiceover:**
  > *"Brought back before the Zamorin after hours of waiting, the atmosphere had turned to ice. The King looked down coldly: 'If your King rules a rich land, why did he send no gold or silver?' Vasco da Gama stood his ground, claiming his voyage was only one of discovery. But behind the throne, the wealthy merchants of Cairo and Mecca were already whispering: these men are not ambassadors... they are pirates."*

The voyage of discovery had just become a battle for survival."*
