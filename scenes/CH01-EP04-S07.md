## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Hostility turns violent. The local authorities attempt to seize the Portuguese pilots and cut off access to fresh water. When Vasco sends two armed longboats to the mainland watering spring at night/dawn, coastal warriors concealed behind wooden palisades and dense mangroves launch a hail of arrows and slingshot stones at the Portuguese boats. Portuguese crossbowmen return fire, driving the defenders back into the trees.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Having identified the Portuguese as European Christians who intended to disrupt their maritime trade, the Sultanate attempted to starve them of fresh water and force their surrender or capture.
