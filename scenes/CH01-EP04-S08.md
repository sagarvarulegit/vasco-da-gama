## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 29 March 1498: Vasco da Gama orders the fleet's departure from Mozambique Island. Under cover of heavy smoke, the armada weighs anchor and sets sail north toward Mombasa, without reliable pilots, entering open waters.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] This dramatic departure marked the transition into the northern Indian Ocean trading network, as the Portuguese navigated unfamiliar waters seeking a pilot who could guide them across the Arabian Sea to India.
