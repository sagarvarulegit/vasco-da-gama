### Panel 1: S01 — The Two Survivors
* **Visual:** Wide shot; *São Gabriel* and *Bérrio* cutting south along the arid yellow cliffs of Africa.
* **Focal Point:** Two lonely weathered wooden caravels sailing through deep blue swell.
* **Color Palette:** Ochre cliffs, deep indigo sea, golden afternoon sunlight.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 2: S02 — The Cape Conquered
* **Visual:** Triumphant wide shot; caravels passing the towering dark cliffs of the Cape of Good Hope into the Atlantic.
* **Focal Point:** Billowing white sails and golden sunlight breaking through clouds.
* **Color Palette:** Dark granite cliffs, brilliant golden clouds, deep cobalt Atlantic.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 3: S03 — The 27-Day Atlantic Flight
* **Visual:** High-speed aerial shot; two ships sailing side-by-side leaving foaming white wakes.
* **Focal Point:** Pure ocean speed under trade wind skies.
* **Color Palette:** Sapphire blue, pure white foam, azure sky.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 4: S04 — The Separation in the Fog
* **Visual:** Moody atmospheric shot; *Bérrio* disappearing into tropical sea fog, leaving *São Gabriel* behind.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco watching the fading silhouette of *Bérrio*.
* **Color Palette:** Silvery-grey fog, dark green sea, damp wood reflections.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 5: S05 — The Illness at Cape Verde
* **Visual:** Chiaroscuro interior; Vasco tending to feverish Paulo in a whitewashed stone room at Santiago.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco's anguished expression and Paulo's blood-stained cloth.
* **Color Palette:** Harsh yellow sunlight through shutters, deep amber shadows.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 6: S06 — The Race for the Azores
* **Visual:** Intimate cabin shot; Vasco holding Paulo's hand as cold North Atlantic waves roll outside the window.
* **Focal Point:** The clasped hands of the two brothers in candlelight.
* **Color Palette:** Chilly grey-green ocean, warm candle amber, dark oak wood.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 7: S07 — The Final Breath
* **Visual:** Devastating interior shot; Paulo dying in Vasco's arms in a stone room at Angra do Heroísmo.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco weeping over his brother's body; friars bowing heads in prayer.
* **Color Palette:** Soft slate-grey rain light, pale skin, dark wool robes.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 8: S08 — Solitude in the Crypt
* **Visual:** Master tragic wide shot; Vasco in black mourning robes kneeling alone before Paulo's tomb in the monastery crypt.
* **Focal Point:** The inscribed stone tomb and tall flickering torches casting long shadows.
* **Color Palette:** Cold grey granite, warm torchlight orange, deep velvety black.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.
