## Episode Objective

Visualize the pivotal turning point of the entire expedition: rounding the southern tip of Africa. The episode balances heavy physical labor (careening fouled hulls), tense cross-cultural encounters (St Helena Bay skirmish), the raw maritime violence of the Cape's gale-force headwinds, the triumphant doubling of the Cape on 22 November 1497, the joyful cross-cultural musical exchange at Mossel Bay, and the strategic reduction of the armada from four vessels to three.

---

## Voice and Narration Rules

* Measured, intimate documentary narration based strictly on the surviving first-voyage journal/account (*Roteiro* / Ravenstein 1898).
* No theatrical melodrama or fabricated dialogue presented as fact.
* Reconstructions and cinematic choices are clearly categorized in production records.
