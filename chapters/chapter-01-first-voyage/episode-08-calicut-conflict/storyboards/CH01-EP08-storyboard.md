### Panel 1: S01 — The Return to the Flagship
* **Visual:** Medium-wide shot; ship's boat cutting through foaming breakers; Vasco da Gama looking back at India with fierce determination.
* **Focal Point:** Da Gama's clenched jaw and the looming silhouette of *São Gabriel*.
* **Color Palette:** Stormy slate-grey sky, white sea foam, dark oak hull.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 2: S02 — The Spices of Malabar
* **Visual:** Warm interior shot; black peppercorns falling onto a gleaming brass scale beside Diogo Dias.
* **Focal Point:** Hands weighing shiny peppercorns and fragrant cinnamon barks.
* **Color Palette:** Warm golden sunbeams, deep brown burlap, bright brass.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 3: S03 — The Arrest of the Factors
* **Visual:** High-tension action shot; Nair warriors with drawn swords storming the warehouse and chaining Diogo Dias.
* **Focal Point:** Clanking iron chains and Arab merchants watching in shadows.
* **Color Palette:** Chiaroscuro shadows, gleaming steel swords, crimson silk turbans.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 4: S04 — The Hostage Ambush
* **Visual:** Dramatic deck shot on *São Gabriel*; Portuguese mariners raising crossbows around 16 trapped Indian nobles.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco da Gama in steel armor signaling from the quarterdeck.
* **Color Palette:** High-contrast midday sun, gleaming steel armor, gold-bordered white silks.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 5: S05 — The Hostage Exchange
* **Visual:** Emotional two-shot at golden sunset; Diogo Dias handing the palm-leaf letter (*olla*) to Vasco.
* **Focal Point:** The inscribed palm-leaf document with ancient Malayalam script.
* **Color Palette:** Golden hour sunset, calm purple coastal waters, glowing orange sky.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 6: S06 — The Naval Bombardment
* **Visual:** Epic naval battle wide shot; *São Gabriel* firing heavy broadsides into a swarm of 70 attacking war boats.
* **Focal Point:** Blinding orange cannon muzzle flashes and splintering attacking craft.
* **Color Palette:** Dark storm clouds, glowing orange fire, thick white smoke.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 7: S07 — Careening at Anjediva
* **Visual:** Wide tropical landscape; *São Gabriel* careened on a white sand beach with sailors scraping barnacles.
* **Focal Point:** The exposed wooden keel and crystal turquoise lagoon waters.
* **Color Palette:** Turquoise sea, emerald palm hills, pristine white sand.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 8: S08 — The Mysterious Gaspar da Gama
* **Visual:** Intimate sunset shot; the European-dressed merchant stepping aboard under armed guard to meet Vasco.
* **Focal Point:** Gaspar's intelligent gaze meeting Vasco da Gama's suspicious eyes.
* **Color Palette:** Amber sunset glow, long deck shadows, polished steel pikes.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.
