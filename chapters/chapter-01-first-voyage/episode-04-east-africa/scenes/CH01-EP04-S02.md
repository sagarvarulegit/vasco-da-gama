## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 11 Jan 1498 fleet reaches a small river. Martin Afonso (interpreter) and a companion go ashore. The local people are tall, peaceful, and hospitable. They bring water and poultry; the women and men wear copper armlets, tin ornaments in their hair, and dagger sheaths of ivory. Vasco names the land *Terra da Boa Gente* (Land of Good People) and the stream *Rio do Cobre* (Copper River).

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] These were southern Bantu-speaking agriculturalist/pastoralist peoples connected to internal African copper trade networks. The peaceful interaction provided desperately needed fresh provisions, water, and morale boost.
