# Video Direction — The Journey as Lived Experience

**Series naming:** Chapter 1 is the overall first-voyage story; its YouTube installments use `CH01-EP##`.

## Creative objective

The viewer should feel as if they are traveling with Vasco da Gama’s expedition, not watching a detached biography or reading a comic. The series moves through the voyage as a sensory, historically grounded experience.

## Visual language

Use MASTER_STYLE_02: cinematic European historical painting, naturalistic oil-paint texture, atmospheric perspective, physically plausible light and weather, lived-in ships and ports, and observational camera movement. Favor long holds, slow tracking, deck-height viewpoints, horizon lines, and environmental details over graphic panels or heroic portraits.

## Narrative position

The camera may occupy the deck, quay, boat, or shoreline near the expedition. It can observe Vasco and the crew from within their working world, but should not invent private thoughts as historical fact. Narration distinguishes `[SOURCE]`, `[HISTORICAL]`, `[RECONSTRUCTION]`, and `[CINEMATIC]` material.

## Sound and pacing

Build scenes from diegetic sound: water, rigging, footsteps, cargo, timber, wind, birds, voices, and weather. Music should support scale and uncertainty without turning the voyage into a fantasy adventure trailer. Allow silence and environmental holds.

## Production implication

The locked Chapter 1 frames remain useful as approved story and continuity references. For the final video, they may be animated with slow camera movement, parallax, environmental motion, and narration. Any newly generated painting-style frame is versioned under MASTER_STYLE_02 and reviewed before becoming canonical.
