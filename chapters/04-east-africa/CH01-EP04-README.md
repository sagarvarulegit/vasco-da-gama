# CH01-EP04 — East Africa & Mozambique: The Edge of the Islamic World

**Status:** Scene Architecture & Blueprint Complete / Ready for Asset Generation  
**Series Structure:** Chapter 1 — First Voyage; Episode 4 — East Africa  
**Historical Period:** December 1497 – March 1498  
**Primary Source:** *A Journal of the First Voyage of Vasco da Gama, 1497–1499* (ed. E. G. Ravenstein, Hakluyt Society, 1898, pp. 15–35)

---

## 1. Scope

The fleet's perilous northward passage along the southeast and East African coast into the Indian Ocean sphere:
1. Passing **Terra do Natal** (Christmas 1497) against the brutal Agulhas current.
2. Peaceful watering and trade at **Terra da Boa Gente / Rio do Cobre** (January 1498).
3. The devastating outbreak of scurvy and hull maintenance at **Rio dos Bons Sinais** (Quelimane/Zambezi delta, Jan–Feb 1498).
4. Entry into the opulent, cosmopolitan Swahili-Arab trading kingdom of **Mozambique Island** (March 1498).
5. The tragic cultural collision, trade insult, water ambush, and the naval cannonade before fleeing north without a pilot.

---

## 2. Fleet & Character States

* **Surviving Armada:** Reduced from 4 vessels to 3 following the sacrifice and burning of the supply ship (*SHIP_ST01*) at Mossel Bay.
  * `SHIP_SG01` (*São Gabriel* — Flagship, Captain-Major Vasco da Gama)
  * `SHIP_SR01` (*São Rafael* — Paulo da Gama)
  * `SHIP_B01` (*Berrio* — Nicolau Coelho)
* **Ship Condition:** Weather-beaten, sun-bleached spars, heavily fouled hulls encrusted with thick green tropical algae and shipworms.
* **Character Continuity (`VASCO_01`):** 8 months into the voyage. Deeply sun-darkened skin, salt-stained linen and worn leather doublet, full weathered dark beard, razor-sharp eyes showing the physical and psychological toll of scurvy among his men and mounting paranoia in foreign waters.

## 3. Scene Breakdown (Netflix/OTT Prestige Docudrama)

1. `CH01-EP04-S01`: **The Black Sickness (Cold Open)** — The horror of scurvy inside the fetid hold of *São Gabriel*.
2. `CH01-EP04-S02`: **The Land of Good People** — Friendly trade and fresh water at Rio do Cobre with copper-wearing Bantu pastoralists.
3. `CH01-EP04-S03`: **The Careen on the Sandbar** — Hauling *São Rafael* onto the sandbank at Rio dos Bons Sinais under scorching tropical sun.
4. `CH01-EP04-S04`: **Signs of the East & The Stone Pillar** — First contact with turbaned merchants wearing silk; erecting the *Padrão de São Rafael*.
5. `CH01-EP04-S05`: **The Coral Kingdom** — Entering the turquoise lagoon of Mozambique Island amid white-sailed Arab dhows and coral-stone minarets.
6. `CH01-EP04-S06`: **The Sultan's Audience & The Trade Insult** — The Sheikh of Mozambique visits the flagship; the insult of cheap European trade goods.
7. `CH01-EP04-S07`: **Ambush in the Shallows** — Desperate skirmish at the mainland water spring amid mangroves and flying poisoned arrows.
8. `CH01-EP04-S08`: **Thunder in the Lagoon (Climax & Cliffhanger)** — Bombarding Mozambique Island with caravel bombards and escaping north into hostile, uncharted seas.
