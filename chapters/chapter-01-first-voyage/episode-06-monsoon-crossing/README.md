## 1. Scope & Historical Narrative

Episode 6 chronicles the pivotal 23-day oceanic sprint across the Arabian Sea (Indian Ocean) from the East African port of Malindi to the historic landfall off Calicut (Kozhikode, India):

1. **Unfurling into the Monsoon (24 April 1498):** Departure from Malindi with the Gujarati pilot (`PILOT_01`) on board; hoisting square sails and catching the raging South-West Monsoon winds.
2. **The Master of the Stars (May 1498):** Celestial navigation using the traditional wooden *kamal* instrument held between the pilot's teeth to sight Polaris.
3. **The Roaring Southwest Monsoon (May 1498):** Battling Force 8 oceanic gales and 25-foot swells, driving the three caravels at an unprecedented 8–9 knots.
4. **The Scurvy Nightmare Below Deck (May 1498):** Scurvy flares up aggressively in the hold of *São Rafael*; Paulo da Gama cares for dying and incapacitated sailors.
5. **Harvesting Monsoon Rain (May 1498):** Euphoric relief as heavy equatorial rain is funneled from canvas sails into empty water casks.
6. **Signs of the Coast (18 May 1498):** Sighting yellow-banded sea snakes, river grass, and floating coconut debris indicating land proximity.
7. **The Mountains of India Emerge (18 May 1498):** The towering green peaks of the Western Ghats (Monte Formoso) pierce through sunset storm clouds.
8. **Dropping Anchor at Calicut (20 May 1498):** Anchoring off Kappad Beach; dozens of local Malayali fishermen and merchant rowboats approach the foreign fleet.
