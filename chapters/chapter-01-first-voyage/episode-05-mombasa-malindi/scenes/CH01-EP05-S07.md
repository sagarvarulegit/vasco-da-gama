## Source Facts

[SOURCE] 18 April 1498: The King of Malindi comes out in a large rowboat (*almadia*) to meet Vasco da Gama on the water. The King sits on a bronze chair cushioned with silk, beneath a crimson damask parasol carried on a pole by an attendant, dressed in a damask robe trimmed with green satin and wearing a rich turban. Vasco da Gama boards his armed skiff dressed in formal European attire. They row alongside each other, exchange gifts, converse through interpreters, and the Portuguese fire bombards and sound trumpets in celebration.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Swahili court protocol placed immense prestige on royal regalia, notably the *siwa* (ceremonial ivory side-blown horns), damask parasols, and silk cushions. Meeting on the water was a mutually agreed neutral zone that preserved both sovereign dignity and military security.
