## 1. Episode Narrative Arc & Rhythm

The narrative represents the decisive geopolitical and navigational hinge of the entire Indian Ocean expedition:

* **Act I: Cold Open & The Treacherous Citadel (0:00 – 1:05)**
  * `S01`: The grounding of *São Rafael* on Mtangata Reef; intense raw physical struggle.
  * `S02`: The sighting of Mombasa's white coral walls; the gift of sweet oranges and the poisoned invitation.
* **Act II: The Ambush & The Night of Terror (1:05 – 1:55)**
  * `S03`: The convicts' tour through Mombasa; tragic misinterpretation of Indian merchants for Christians.
  * `S04`: The sudden grounding panic at the harbor mouth; pilots leaping into the sea as the anchor crashes.
  * `S05`: Midnight cable-cutting swimmers and the chilling drop-torture interrogation on the dark deck.
* **Act III: The Haven & The Alliance (1:55 – 2:35)**
  * `S06`: Easter Sunday arrival at Malindi; relief, fresh spices, and open hospitality.
  * `S07`: The historic floating royal summit between Vasco da Gama and the Sultan of Malindi.
* **Act IV: The Pilot & The Final Crossing (2:35 – 3:15)**
  * `S08`: Securing the Gujarati master pilot (`PILOT_01`), raising the *Padrão de São Rafael*, and the epic launch into the open Arabian Sea.
