## Source Facts

[SOURCE] Tropical rain squalls are characteristic of the intertropical convergence zone and monsoon transitions in the Indian Ocean, providing critical replenishment of fresh water during long ocean passages.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] On long voyages, water stored in wooden casks quickly deteriorated, becoming infested with worms and foul odors. Fresh rain harvested from sails was literally the difference between life and death for 15th-century mariners.

## Reconstruction

[RECONSTRUCTION] The collective effort of the crew utilizing spare sails and tarpaulins to funnel torrential rain into the ship's water casks.
