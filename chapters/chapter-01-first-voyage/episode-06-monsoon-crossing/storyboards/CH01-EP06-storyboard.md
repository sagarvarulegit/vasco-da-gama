### Panel 1: S01 — The Great Sprint Begins
* **Visual:** Dynamic side-profile of *São Gabriel* surging into open blue sea from Malindi, sails swelling.
* **Focal Point:** Red crosses on white canvas catching wind; Vasco and the Gujarati pilot standing on quarterdeck.
* **Color Palette:** Turquoise shallows transitioning to deep cobalt, brilliant African morning light.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 2: S02 — The Starlight Navigator
* **Visual:** Intimate medium shot of PILOT_01 holding the wooden *kamal* plate in mouth/hand, sighting Polaris.
* **Focal Point:** Silhouette of the instrument against the glowing North Star and southern constellation field.
* **Color Palette:** Deep indigo night, cold silvery moonlight, glowing bioluminescent blue water.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 3: S03 — The Fury of the Southwest Monsoon
* **Visual:** Low-angle heroic action shot of the caravels heeling in 25ft emerald waves.
* **Focal Point:** Crashing white bow spray, taut rigging, drenched sailors holding fast.
* **Color Palette:** Storm grey, emerald sea foam, stormy shafts of sunlight.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 4: S04 — The Agony of the Crew
* **Visual:** Moody chiaroscuro interior of São Rafael’s hold; Paulo da Gama tending to scurvy victims.
* **Focal Point:** Swaying lantern light illuminating pale faces, swollen gums, rough burlap bedding.
* **Color Palette:** Warm amber lantern glow, deep shadow, damp weathered oak.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 5: S05 — Rain of Deliverance
* **Visual:** Medium wide shot of joyful mariners catching cascading monsoon downpour in open casks.
* **Focal Point:** Crew tilting heads back to drink fresh rain; steam rising from deck wood.
* **Color Palette:** Wet silvery-slate rain tones, glossy wet wood reflections.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 6: S06 — The Living Signs of India
* **Visual:** Over-the-shoulder shot from bowsprit; pilot pointing to floating river grass and sea snakes.
* **Focal Point:** Yellow-banded sea snakes gliding past drifting coconut husks in turquoise water.
* **Color Palette:** Golden afternoon sun, vibrant green river weeds, clear water.
* **Duration:** 15–20 sec.

### Panel 7: S07 — The Mountains of India Emerge
* **Visual:** Epic widescreen panorama of the towering green Western Ghats rising through sunset mist.
* **Focal Point:** Sailors cheering in the rigging; three ships sailing in formation toward the giant peaks.
* **Color Palette:** Sunset gold, violet storm clouds, deep emerald mountain slopes.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.

### Panel 8: S08 — Anchor Down at Calicut
* **Visual:** Master establishing cliffhanger shot; the three caravels anchored off Kappad beach surrounded by native Indian outrigger boats.
* **Focal Point:** Vasco da Gama in armor on quarterdeck observing dozens of Malayali boats approaching.
* **Color Palette:** Lush tropical green palms, white sands, calm jade coastal waters.
* **Duration:** 20–25 sec.
