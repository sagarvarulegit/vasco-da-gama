# ZAMORIN_01 — Samutiri Raja (The King of Calicut)

**Reference status:** APPROVED / CANONICAL BASELINE (1498)

- **Role:** Hereditary Hindu monarch of the Kingdom of Kozhikode (Calicut), presiding over the wealthiest entrepôt of the Malabar spice trade.
- **Age:** Middle-aged (40s–50s in 1498).
- **Face/build:** Regal, composed, authoritative posture; distinguished South Asian Malayali features, dark expressive eyes.
- **Skin/hair:** South Asian skin tone, groomed dark hair and beard or mustache as per traditional Malabar royal custom.
- **Clothing:** Traditional Kerala royal regalia: draped in sheer white Malabar muslin with woven gold borders (*kasavu*), bare upper torso adorned with cascading pearl necklaces and large emerald and ruby pendants. Enormous emerald earrings the size of walnuts and heavy diamond-encrusted gold rings.
- **Accessories/Court Rituals:** Attendants holding a solid gold spittoon, betel nut leaves (*paan*) on golden platters, silver-inlaid royal fans, and green velvet daybeds/cushions.
- **Personality cues:** Formidable sovereign, observant, commercially astute, diplomatic yet unyielding in the face of perceived disrespect or meager trade offerings.
- **Known facts:** Eyewitness description in the *Roteiro* of the audience on 28 May 1498 in his stone palace in Calicut.
- **Uncertain/speculative:** Exact personal name of the ruling Samutiri in May 1498 (succession titles followed the dynastic line).
