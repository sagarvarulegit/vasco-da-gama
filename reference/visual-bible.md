# Visual Bible — MASTER_STYLE_01 / MASTER_STYLE_02

## Core style

MASTER_STYLE_01 is a cinematic historical graphic novel: detailed painterly environments, controlled natural linework, realistic anatomy and skin texture, historically plausible late-fifteenth-century clothing, physically plausible light, atmospheric perspective, weathered wood/rope/sail/metal, and subtle filmic depth. The world is lived-in, practical, and maritime. No glossy CGI, fantasy armor, heroic posing, or modern visual elements.

## Historical lens

The Portuguese expedition enters an already-connected Indian Ocean world. Ports in East Africa and Malabar are populated, commercial, multilingual, and politically organized. Frame Portuguese uncertainty as their limitation, not as evidence that the places are empty or primitive.

## Palette and light

Atlantic: desaturated blue, wet timber, cold cloud and hard glare. Cape: slate water and wind-scoured ochre. East Africa: warm coral, mangrove green, humid haze. Malabar: monsoon cloud, deep green, laterite red, packed port color. Use natural firelight and sun; avoid fantasy color grading.

## Continuity rules

- Use permanent Character IDs and Ship IDs in every prompt.
- Vasco (VASCO_01) ages through exposure: maintained at departure, salt-stained and bearded mid-voyage, exhausted and thinner on return.
- Ships remain heavy, imperfect working vessels with stable silhouettes and damage progression.
- Clothing changes by climate, wear, and voyage duration; never reset to departure condition.
- Architecture, dress, tools, and people must be region-specific and labeled by evidence level.

## Prompt contract

Every image prompt names era, date/period, location, IDs, clothing state, environment, light, camera/composition, emotional tone, restrictions, and MASTER_STYLE_01. Canonical images require historical and continuity review before lock.

## Approved visual benchmark

**CH01-S01-v1** is the first approved exemplar of MASTER_STYLE_01. Preserve its detailed painterly surfaces, weathered wood and rope texture, soft atmospheric depth, natural summer light, grounded human scale, and restrained graphic-novel linework. Treat the painting style as canonical while continuing to correct any scene-specific historical errors.

## Style evolution — MASTER_STYLE_02

The project owner has approved a shift away from comic-panel visual language toward an immersive European historical-painting treatment for the video series. This is a deliberate evolution, not a silent change to MASTER_STYLE_01.

**MASTER_STYLE_02 characteristics:**

- cinematic European historical painting
- naturalistic oil-paint surface and atmospheric depth
- convincing late-fifteenth-century human anatomy, clothing, ships, and environments
- broad painterly masses with selective fine detail rather than graphic-novel outlines
- physically plausible light, weather, haze, water, smoke, and fire
- observational camera language that places the viewer within the expedition
- restrained composition; no poster-like heroic posing or comic-panel exaggeration
- no glossy CGI, fantasy medievalism, modern visual elements, or anachronistic architecture

**Camera lens language (`visual-skills` standard):**
- **24mm / 28mm (Wide / Environmental Pressure):** Used for wide establishing scale, turbulent seas, and expansive port panoramas (e.g. Mozambique Island lagoon, sandbar careening).
- **35mm (Medium Wide / Staging & Group Dynamic):** Used for human interactions, quarterdeck council, and shore barter encounters.
- **50mm (Natural Human Eye / Emotional Truth):** Used for two-shots and direct character confrontations (e.g. Sheikh audience, Vasco kneeling in hold).
- **85mm / 100mm (Close-up & Macro / Emotional Climax):** Used for high-stakes micro-details (e.g. vinegar swab, clenched knuckles on rapier, whistling arrow impact).

**The 3 Physical Details Law (Mandatory Prompt Requirement):**
Every image prompt must specify 3 concrete physical facts instead of empty adjectives (`epic`, `cinematic`, `masterpiece`):
1. **Environmental Pressure:** Concrete physical element (choking pitch smoke, humid bilge air, scorching sun glare on limestone).
2. **Body Micro-Action:** Emotion carried in anatomy (jaw locked rigid, salt-cracked knuckles, eyes narrowed against spray).
3. **Sound / Texture Anchor:** Tactile surface detail (wet weathered oak, rusted iron bolts, coarse linen).

**Viewer experience:** The audience should feel present aboard the expedition—hearing rigging, smelling salt and wet timber, sharing heat, fatigue, uncertainty, and changing weather—while traveling through historically grounded locations with Vasco’s fleet.

**Continuity rule:** CH01-LOCK-v1 remains approved under its documented benchmark. New scenes and video shots use MASTER_STYLE_02. Any Chapter 1 re-render must be versioned separately and must not overwrite the locked images.
