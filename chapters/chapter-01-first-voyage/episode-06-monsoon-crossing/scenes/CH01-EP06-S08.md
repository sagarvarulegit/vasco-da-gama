## Source Facts

[SOURCE] On Sunday, 20 May 1498, the Portuguese fleet cast anchor about two leagues below the city of Calicut (at Kappad). Almost immediately, local boats (*almadies*) rowed out to the ships to inspect the newcomers and ask who they were.

## Historical Context

[HISTORICAL] Calicut (Kozhikode) was the premier international trade emporium of the Indian Ocean, governed by the Hindu monarch known as the *Samudri Raja* (Zamorin). It was home to thriving communities of Arab, Persian, Jewish, Gujarati, and Malayali merchants dealing in black pepper, ginger, cinnamon, and silk.
